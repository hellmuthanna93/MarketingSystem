# Content writing — Anna Hellmuth

## First step

Read **[brand/marketing/content.md](brand/marketing/content.md)** and **[brand/marketing/messaging.md](brand/marketing/messaging.md)**.

For CTAs and the humanized-writing rules: **[brand/voice.md](brand/voice.md)**.

## Rules

1. **Sell movement, not process.** Lead with relief, clarity, confidence, transformation.
2. **Apply the checklist** before finalizing (relevance, specificity, authenticity, emotional connection, depth, business value)
3. **Use the formula:** You think X. Actually, Y. That is why Z.
4. **Tone:** from experience, as an equal. Warm, professional, honest; not academic or pushy.
5. **Pick a lane:** counseling (past/emotional) or coaching (future/goals), or state both if relevant
6. **Sources:** lived experience, genuine learnings, anonymized client patterns, not generic self-help
7. **CTAs:** soft invitation only; primary = "Schedule your discovery call". No guilt or urgency.
8. **HTML social posts:** use `projects/social/templates/` + `brand/*.css` (no hardcoded hex)
9. **Humanize every draft.** Follow the "Humanized writing (no AI tells)" rules in `brand/voice.md`. Hardest rule: **never use em dashes or en dashes (`—` `–`)**; use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses instead. Also cut AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry, underscore, showcase, foster), forced rule-of-three, "not just X but Y" parallelisms, "serves as / stands as" copula avoidance, signposting ("let's dive in"), and generic upbeat conclusions. Scan for `—` and `–` before publishing.

## Weekly rhythm

Minimum: 1 counseling post + 1 coaching post per week. See topic lists in `brand/marketing/content.md`.

## Before / after

**Weak:** "As a medical doctor and psychologist, I use integrative methods including CBT and hypnotherapy to help clients achieve better mental health."

**Strong:** "You can explain your patterns perfectly and still feel stuck. Understanding isn't the same as release. That's often when people realize they need support going deeper than insight alone."

**Weak:** "Book now, limited spots! Transform your life in 6 sessions guaranteed!"

**Strong:** "You don't have to figure this out alone. If something in this resonates, a discovery call is a calm place to explore whether we're a good fit."

## Output

- Match platform length (Instagram: tight; LinkedIn: can expand insight)
- One clear insight per post
- Optional soft CTA at end. Never required in every post.
