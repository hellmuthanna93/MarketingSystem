# Content writing - Anna Hellmuth

## First step

Read **[brand/marketing/content.md](brand/marketing/content.md)** and **[brand/marketing/messaging.md](brand/marketing/messaging.md)**.

For CTAs and the humanized-writing rules: **[brand/voice.md](brand/voice.md)**.

## Rules

1. **Sell movement, not process.** Lead with relief, clarity, confidence, transformation.
2. **Apply the checklist** before finalizing (relevance, specificity, authenticity, emotional connection, depth, business value)
3. **Use the formula:** You think X. Actually, Y. That is why Z.
4. **Tone:** from experience, as an equal. Warm, professional, honest; not academic or pushy.
5. **Pick a lane:** counseling (past/emotional) or coaching (future/goals), or state both if relevant
6. **Sources:** lived experience, genuine learnings, anonymized client patterns, not generic self-help
7. **CTAs:** soft invitation only; primary = "Schedule your discovery call". No guilt or urgency.
8. **HTML social posts:** use `projects/social/templates/` + `brand/*.css` (no hardcoded hex)
9. **Humanize every draft.** Follow the "Humanized writing (no AI tells)" rules in `brand/voice.md`. Hardest rule: **use regular hyphens only.** Never use Unicode em or en dash characters. Use a regular hyphen (`-`) when a dash is helpful, or choose a period, comma, colon, parentheses, or a restructured sentence. Also cut AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry, underscore, showcase, foster), forced rule-of-three, "not just X but Y" parallelisms, "serves as / stands as" copula avoidance, signposting ("let's dive in"), and generic upbeat conclusions. Scan for Unicode dash characters before publishing.

## Anna's personal content principles

The purpose of my content is to show how I think. It should:

- make deep observations,
- name things that other people can't articulate,
- connect theory with real life,
- explain the "why" behind patterns.

**Capture ideas immediately.** Write or record as soon as an idea appears: after a client session, after I learn something, from my own stories and observations.

**Make my work obvious.** Invite people to work with me clearly. Readers should think, "This is manageable. This is exactly what I need help with." Translate the value of my work into language people understand.

**Communicate my values and beliefs.** The main goal is teaching and bringing value. I want people to understand not just the information but how I see the world.

**Know who I'm speaking to:** deep thinkers, high achievers, immigrants, people in transition, successful people who don't feel fulfilled, people with internal blocks, people who want more satisfaction in life.

**The experience I want to create:** readers understand themselves and life more deeply, exhale, realize nothing is fundamentally wrong with them, feel accepted and seen, leave with useful ideas and practical tools, and naturally become interested in working with me.

**Reminders for myself:**

- I am already deep enough. I don't need to overcomplicate things.
- I am not my content. Creating content is one of my roles. It exists to serve my goals and my business, not to define who I am.
- There are enough successful specialists whose English is worse than mine.

## Weekly rhythm

Minimum: 1 counseling post + 1 coaching post per week. See topic lists in `brand/marketing/content.md`.

## Before / after

**Weak:** "As a medical doctor and psychologist, I use integrative methods including CBT and hypnotherapy to help clients achieve better mental health."

**Strong:** "You can explain your patterns perfectly and still feel stuck. Understanding isn't the same as release. That's often when people realize they need support going deeper than insight alone."

**Weak:** "Book now, limited spots! Transform your life in 6 sessions guaranteed!"

**Strong:** "You don't have to figure this out alone. If something in this resonates, a discovery call is a calm place to explore whether we're a good fit."

## Output

- Match platform length (Instagram: tight; LinkedIn: can expand insight)
- One clear insight per post
- Optional soft CTA at end. Never required in every post.
