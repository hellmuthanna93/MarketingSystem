# DACH mental-health compliance — Anna Hellmuth

Guardrails so marketing copy stays compliant in Germany/DACH. **Not legal advice** — when uncertain, mark TBD and recommend Anna confirm with a Rechtsanwalt. Full doc: `business-site/docs/07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md` and `business-site/docs/04-marketing/06-outreach-compliance-germany.md`.

## Scope: Beratung vs Psychotherapie (most important)

- Anna offers **psychological counseling (Beratung) and coaching** — NOT Psychotherapie under the Psychotherapeutengesetz unless she holds Approbation/Heilpraktiker für Psychotherapie status (confirm — see profile).
- **Never market as:** "Psychotherapie", "treating mental illness", "Heilung von Depression", diagnosing, or curing disorders, unless the legal basis is confirmed.
- **Do market as:** counseling, support, accompaniment, working through patterns, emotional support, coaching toward goals.
- Symptom language ("mild-to-moderate depression", "anxiety") is fine as **audience recognition** in content, but copy must not promise to **treat or cure** clinical conditions.

## HWG (Heilmittelwerbegesetz) — advertising for health services

- Anna is a medical doctor; advertising health-adjacent services carries HWG sensitivity.
- **Avoid:** before/after promises, guaranteed healing, fear-based advertising, misleading success claims, "results guaranteed".
- **Avoid:** testimonials that claim cure of a specific illness (HWG §11 historically restricts certain Werbung with Gutachten/Empfehlungen and patient stories for medical advertising) — keep testimonials about *experience and process*, anonymous, not disease-cure claims.
- When in doubt about a health claim, soften to experience/possibility language and flag for legal review.

## UWG §7 — outreach / unsolicited contact

- **No cold email or cold calls** to individuals without prior consent (opt-in).
- Warm/inbound only: people who follow, message, or request contact.
- Newsletter requires **double opt-in** with clear consent and unsubscribe.

## GDPR / DSGVO — mental-health data is special category

- Health data = Art. 9 special category; needs explicit consent and strong safeguards.
- Tools that process client data (calendar, video, notes, payment) need an **Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV/DPA)**; prefer EU-hosted or DPA-covered providers.
- Privacy policy must state what is collected, why, retention, and rights.
- Never quote identifiable client material in marketing.

## Quick gate for any claim

1. Does it imply Psychotherapie/treatment/cure? → reframe as counseling/support, or flag.
2. Does it guarantee an outcome? → remove.
3. Does it use a patient story to claim a cure? → reframe to anonymous experience/process.
4. Is it outreach to someone who didn't opt in? → don't send.
5. Does a tool touch client data without a DPA? → flag.

This skill is conservative by design. Prefer possibility/experience language; route hard legal questions to a lawyer.
