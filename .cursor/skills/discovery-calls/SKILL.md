# Discovery calls — Anna Hellmuth

The discovery call is a **free 30-minute mutual fit check, not therapy**. Goal: help the person understand their situation, show a possible future, name what keeps them stuck, and invite a next step. Respect and freedom over persuasion.

Read `practice-voice` and `practice-profile` first. Full script lives at `business-site/docs/09-toolkit/01-discovery-call-script.md`.

## The flow (30 min)

1. **Welcome & frame (2 min)** — "This is a free, no-obligation half hour to see whether we're a good fit. There's nothing you need to prepare."
2. **What brings you here (8–10 min)** — open question, then listen. Do not fix yet.
3. **Name the pattern (5–7 min)** — reflect back the inner conflict precisely. This is where trust is earned: *"finally someone understands."*
4. **Show the possible future (4 min)** — what becomes possible with support; honest about the work involved.
5. **Lane & path (4 min)** — counseling vs coaching; package vs (proposed) Clarity Session wedge.
6. **Practical & close (3 min)** — format, frequency, pricing; invite the next step without pressure.

## Listen-fors (qualification)

- **Counseling signals:** depression, anxiety, overthinking, shame, attachment/relationship fear, body image, burnout, expat displacement, "understanding hasn't been enough"
- **Coaching signals:** stuck on goals, fear/paralysis, identity change, transition, "I know what I want but can't act"
- **Both / unclear:** clarity itself can be the goal — recommend starting where the pressure is loudest
- **Not a fit:** wants guaranteed outcomes, quick fixes without inner work, motivation-only — name limits honestly

## Lane decision

| If they say… | Lane |
|--------------|------|
| "I can't move past / I keep carrying…" | Counseling |
| "I want to / I'm trying to but can't act…" | Coaching |
| "I don't know where to start" | Start where pressure is loudest; clarity is valid |

## Path recommendation

- **Clear, committed, knows the work:** counseling 6-pack (€660) or coaching package (€1,020 / €1,920)
- **Wants to experience the depth first, low commitment:** (proposed) Clarity Session wedge — see `practice-profile` for status
- **Single specific issue:** single session (€120 counseling / €180 coaching)

## Close (no pressure)

- Adults decide for themselves. No "limited spots", no urgency.
- "Take whatever time you need. If it feels right, here's how we'd start. If not, that's completely okay."
- Offer to send a short follow-up email with the recommendation and link.

## Never

- Diagnose on the call (Beratung scope — see `dach-mental-health-compliance`)
- Promise outcomes
- Pressure to decide on the spot
