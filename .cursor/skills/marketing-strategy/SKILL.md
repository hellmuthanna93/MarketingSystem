# Marketing strategy — Anna Hellmuth

## First step

Read **[brand/marketing/positioning.md](brand/marketing/positioning.md)**, **[brand/marketing/audience.md](brand/marketing/audience.md)**, and **[brand/marketing/sales.md](brand/marketing/sales.md)**.

For approved copy and taglines: **[brand/marketing/messaging.md](brand/marketing/messaging.md)**.

## Business context

- **Vision:** profitable online practice with freedom, depth, meaningful work, sustainable lifestyle
- **5-year goal:** €300k income, 6–7 hrs/day max, 2 free days/week, high-quality clients over volume
- **2026 priorities:** consistent content, audience trust, coaching clients, positioning, systems, wellbeing

## Positioning (non-negotiable)

- Not selling information — selling **lasting inner change**
- People buy **movement** (relief, hope, clarity, confidence, possibility, transformation)
- Guiding principle: ideal clients think *"Finally someone understands what I have been experiencing"* — and feel *she knows what she's doing; I can trust her; we'll get where I need to go*
- Strength: psychological depth + coaching practicality + pattern recognition + warmth + **lived experience** — you do not speak from above; you have been there

## Audience

- **Profile:** sensitive, deep-feeling people who understand a lot and strive for change — often highly educated professionals functioning externally, stuck internally; insight alone has not been enough
- **Public label:** *sensitive, thoughtful souls* (use in new content; live site hero still uses legacy *creative & ambitious souls*)
- **Counseling:** emotional/past-focused — heal, release, regulate
- **Coaching:** goal/future-focused — action, purpose, transitions, authentic change

## Sales approach

- Do not convince, push, or pressure
- Help understand situation → show possible future → explain what keeps stuck → invite next step
- Pricing: no apology, no excessive justification; adults decide for themselves
- Attraction levers: trust, visibility, consistency, depth

## Facts (do not contradict)

Pricing, services, credentials, and CTAs must match **[brand/marketing/sales.md](brand/marketing/sales.md)** and **[brand/marketing/messaging.md](brand/marketing/messaging.md)**.

Primary CTA: **Schedule your discovery call** → `https://calendar.app.google/ueKb9RbyxWyC6zfK8`

## Recommendations style

- Prefer depth + consistency over volume
- New marketing content: outcomes first; methods/credentials on trust pages only
- One powerful post > twenty generic posts
- Align campaigns with work-life balance goals — no hustle framing
