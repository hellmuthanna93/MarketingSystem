# Practice voice — Anna Hellmuth

The quality and consistency engine for all copy. Read this and [humanizer-patterns.md](humanizer-patterns.md) before drafting anything a client or prospect will read, then layer `practice-profile` for facts.

Canonical source: [brand/voice.md](brand/voice.md) · [brand/marketing/content.md](brand/marketing/content.md) · [brand/marketing/messaging.md](brand/marketing/messaging.md).

## Voice in one line

Warm and understanding, **and** unmistakably confident that inner states can change — a specialist who names the reader's experience precisely, has been there too, and stays grounded as the one who can help them move.

## Voice attributes

- **Felt inner strength and compassion** — steady, grounded presence; firmness and care at once
- **Not separate from the struggle** — the reader feels you know it from the inside, not from a distance
- **Precise recognition** — name the inner conflict, patterns, and stuck points so exactly the reader thinks *"this is written about me"*
- **Grounded certainty, not hype** — change takes work; paralysis is not permanent; the path is knowable
- **Respects grown-ups** — readers are intelligent adults who hold complexity and decide for themselves
- **Honest about limits** — no guaranteed outcomes, fully confident in the process

## Signature move (use in most posts)

Lead with **recognition**, then **confidence**, then the **way through**:

> You think X. Actually, Y is happening. That is why Z feels so difficult.

Example (counseling):
> You think you should be "over it" by now. Actually, your body is still holding what your mind tried to leave behind. That is why certain situations still catch you off guard.

## Never-list (hard no-goes)

Never sound like:

- **Motivational slogans** — "you've got this!", "good vibes only", "manifest your dream life"
- **Downplaying** — "it's not that bad", "everyone feels this way", "just think positive"
- **Pity / collapsing into helplessness** — over-sympathizing, treating the reader as a victim, sinking into the stuckness
- **Making yourself small** — apologetic hedging, qualifying disclaimers, shrinking from expertise
- **Parent-to-child voice** — talking down, coddling, infantilizing
- **Coldness or showing off** — clinical distance, academic posturing, jargon as performance
- **Trying to impress** — name-dropping methods, credentials, or theory to look credible
- **Pressure** — "limited spots", "act now", "don't miss out"
- **Over-academic openings** — "Research shows…", "As a physician…"

## Words to prefer

transformation, inner change, clarity, relief, alignment, courageous/brave (coaching), authentic, patterns, stuck, roadblocks, stepping stones, heal, release, fulfill, ascend, discovery call, resonate, mutual fit.

## Words to avoid

guaranteed results, miracle, fix you, hustle, grind, 10x, patient/disorder-first framing in marketing, "just think positive", "manifest your dreams", "limited spots".

## CTAs (soft only)

- Primary: **Schedule your discovery call** → https://calendar.app.google/ueKb9RbyxWyC6zfK8
- Secondary: Discover Counseling Services / Explore Coaching Details
- Tertiary: Learn more about me
- Never required in every post; invitation, not pressure: *"Why not give it a try and find out?"*

## Lane discipline

Pick a lane per piece, or state both if relevant:

- **Counseling** — past/emotional: heal, release, regulate, attachment, shame, body image, depression, expat loneliness
- **Coaching** — future/goals: action, purpose, identity change, transitions, authentic success, paralysis, fear

## Quick self-check (run before finalizing)

1. Would an ideal client think *"finally, someone understands what I experience"*?
2. Did I lead with the **change/recognition**, not the method or credentials?
3. Is there exactly **one** clear insight (not a list of tips)?
4. Did I avoid every item on the never-list?
5. Does it sound like a grounded specialist who has been there — not a cheerleader, not a textbook?
6. Did the humanizer pass remove AI tells? (see humanizer-patterns.md)
7. Are all facts/prices from `practice-profile` (no inventions)?

## Multilingual

EN primary. DE / UK / RU: adapt idioms, keep the same warmth and grounded certainty; test Cyrillic and longer German compounds in templates before publishing. Translate CTA meaning, not word-for-word.
