Getting started

Signs you are carrying more than you should have to alone

By Anna Hellmuth · April 2026 · 6 min read

Self-reliance got you far. It may also be the reason you keep waiting until you break before you ask for help.

You think asking for support means you failed. Actually, it often means you are finally honest about the weight you have been holding, and tired of pretending it is manageable.

There are seasons when self-reliance is enough. There are seasons when a skilled professional saves you years of circular struggle and guides you to insights you cannot reach alone. The difficulty is telling which season you are in.

When "push through" stops working

Consider whether any of these feel familiar:

  • You have tried books, podcasts, and journaling, and the same patterns return
  • You can explain your issues clearly but still feel stuck in them
  • Relationships, sleep, or focus are suffering despite your effort
  • You minimize your pain because others "have it worse"
  • You dread being alone with your thoughts
  • A major transition (move, breakup, career shift) has unsettled your foundation

These are not weaknesses. They are signals that the problem may be deeper or more layered than willpower alone can address.

The cost of waiting

We are our own toughest critics, and our own most persistent roadblocks. Without support, people often repeat the same relationship dynamics, career misalignments, or emotional spirals for years, each time hoping insight will be enough.

With the right professional: one who truly aligns with your needs, progress can be remarkably faster and lighter. That is not marketing language. It is what I hear from clients who finally found a fit after struggling alone or with the wrong match.

What professional support offers that self-help cannot

A trained counselor or coach provides:

  • A confidential space without performance or judgment
  • Reflection tailored to your patterns, not generic advice
  • Accountability and structure when motivation oscillates
  • Tools for regulation when emotions overwhelm reason
  • Witnessing: the experience of being fully heard, often for the first time

I cannot guarantee a specific outcome; no ethical professional can. I can offer resources, expertise, and steady presence while you do the work, and in my experience, the clients who show up fully and integrate insights between sessions see the most dramatic change.

Trust your intuition about fit

Finding the right person matters as much as finding the right method. If we speak and something in you relaxes, a sense of "she gets it", pay attention. If not, honor that too and keep searching. The goal is your wellbeing, not a sale.
