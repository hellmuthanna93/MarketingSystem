For when understanding your problem does not help, and you still feel anxious, lonely, stuck, and exhausted

Psychological counseling is private online support for sensitive, thoughtful people who understand a lot about themselves, but still feel caught in anxiety, emotional exhaustion, shame, attachment pain, or old patterns they cannot shift alone.

If you look capable from the outside while feeling overwhelmed, empty, anxious, or deeply alone inside, counseling can help you understand what is happening underneath. Maybe you have read, reflected, analyzed, journaled, talked to friends, or tried to explain your past many times.

But the knowledge has not brought relief.

If you feel this

Counseling can be a place to begin when you recognize yourself in one or more of these experiences.

Anxiety that reduces your quality of life.

Relationship problems, feeling unsatisfied in your current relationship, or loneliness without a partner.

Perfectionism, overthinking, imposter syndrome, and not taking action on plans that matter to you.

Lack of joy, emotional heaviness, loss of motivation, numbness, hopeless thoughts, or the feeling that life has become flat and meaningless.

Traumatic or stressful experiences from the past that you feel still influence you.

Not liking your body or personality, or only being able to accept yourself when you perform at a certain level and reach specific achievements.

  • • Being unable to relax properly and unable to work effectively.
  • • The sense that your life does not really feel like yours.

Possible results of counseling

Counseling can help you move from understanding your patterns intellectually to relating to yourself and your life differently.

  • • Understand yourself better and recognize your typical patterns.
  • • Reduce anxiety and become more stable, grounded, and calm in everyday life.
  • • Get out of perfectionism and overthinking, and start acting with more ease.
  • • Find reasons to live and grow again, and feel more fulfilled in life.
  • • Become more satisfied with yourself, your body, and your personality.
  • • Build relationships that feel more emotionally safe, honest, and fulfilling.
  • • Work with traumatic experiences and begin freeing yourself from emotional burdens you have carried for too long.
  • • Work with transgenerational trauma, so you and your children can live a different life than the one your parents were able to live.
  • • Find your place and your people in this world, whether you immigrated, moved to a new city, or stayed where you were born but still feel that nothing around you truly fits.

How sessions work

Counseling is not a fixed program or curriculum. Each session is shaped around what you bring, what feels most important now, and what is realistically possible for your emotional state and life situation.

In sessions, we talk honestly about what is happening in your inner world and your relationships. I help you see patterns more clearly, connect thoughts with feelings, understand where certain reactions come from, and find a way to respond to yourself and your life differently.

I work in an integrative way, combining different psychological approaches depending on what is useful for your situation.

Some sessions are reflective and emotional. Others are more practical, focused on a concrete situation, decision, conversation, or next step. When useful, sessions can also include exercises or guided meditations to help you experience and integrate the work more deeply.

Frequency

Session frequency is individual. The standard rhythm is once a week. In a crisis or more acute phase, we can meet more intensively for a period of time. In a stabilization phase, we can move to a biweekly schedule.

All sessions are online and 60 minutes. Sessions are available in English, German, Ukrainian, Russian, or a mix. Payment, or the first agreed installment, is due upon booking. Cancellation or rescheduling is free up to 48 hours before the appointment; the full fee applies within 48 hours.

Fit and next step

Psychological counseling is a good fit when your main need is healing-oriented: emotional burdens, anxiety, shame, attachment patterns, relationship pain, body image, burnout recovery, or the past still shaping the present.

Please note that I am based and registered in Germany. The online counseling services provided here do not constitute psychotherapy under German psychotherapy regulations. Additionally, online counseling may not be suitable for all situations. Certain issues, particularly acute psychological crises and severe mental health disorders, require in-person and long-term support. In such cases, please reach out to a licensed psychotherapist or emergency services in your area.

The first step is a free 30-minute discovery call to understand the situation, clarify whether counseling is the right form of support, and decide whether working together feels like a mutual fit.
