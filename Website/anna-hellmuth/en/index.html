Knowledge alone is not the same as real change.

Online psychological counseling and life coaching, wherever you are, in English, German, Ukrainian, Russian, or a mix of them all

“I know myself and my typical patterns. I understand my struggles. Actually, I should know better and be able to act differently.”

Is that what you keep thinking about yourself, yet you still find yourself reliving the same patterns over and over again?

Knowledge by itself is only one piece of the puzzle.

And one piece is not enough to see the whole picture. In my work with clients, we complete all the other pieces.

Schedule your discovery call

Many of my clients arrive already highly self-aware. They have read the books, listened to the podcasts, reflected deeply, and often spent years trying to understand themselves. Some have even shamed themselves into knowing better and discovered that shame doesn't work either. Some went further still, into disappointment and even quiet despair, until they stopped trying.

The problem is not a lack of insight.

And there is absolutely nothing wrong with your understanding. You are smart enough, capable enough, and conscientious enough.

The problem is that lasting change requires more than insight alone.

It needs emotional processing, recognition of subtle subconscious patterns, and a shift in your typical inner state. These are things that are almost impossible to do by yourself.

Exactly that is my daily work and great passion in life: to dive deep with you and come back stronger, happier, and healthier.

I would be honored if you entrusted me to guide you on that journey as well.

Frequently asked questions

Can I not address my inner issues on my own? Do I really need someone else's help?
There are times when self-reliance is sufficient, and times when it's not. A skilled specialist can save you years of wasted time and unnecessary struggle, guiding you to insights you might never achieve independently.
What’s the difference between psychological counseling and life coaching? How do I identify my needs?

Life coaching and psychological counseling are both wonderful tools for personal development. They both help you understand yourself better and they both have therapeutic potential, but they differ in focus and approach.

  • Psychological Counseling: Focuses on healing emotional issues and mental health.
  • Life Coaching: Focuses on achieving goals and creating a structured path toward success.

Ask yourself: Are my issues emotional/past-focused (counseling), or goal/future-focused (coaching)? If you're still not sure what you need, just reach out to me.

What methods are you using in your work?
I utilize an integrative approach, customizing my methods to meet your unique needs. My foundation is a humanistic approach, often enhanced with hypnotherapy, which has proven highly effective for many clients. Based on your personality and goals, I may also incorporate psychodynamic therapy, CBT, or systemic constellations. I select methods that will best serve your individual journey.
Can you guarantee the results I'm looking for?
While I can't promise a specific result, I can assure you that I'll provide you with the resources and support necessary for your growth. In my experience, the most transformative results come from clients who are fully present in each session and integrate their insights into daily life. These are the individuals whose lives have seen dramatic change.
I work 9 to 5. Can I book evening or weekend appointments?
For optimal effectiveness, I recommend scheduling sessions when you are fresh and well-rested. However, I understand that time zone differences and work schedules can be challenging. Therefore, I do offer some evening and weekend slots.
What happens if I have to cancel an appointment?
Sessions can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled appointment. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice will incur the full session fee.
Can we work together if I'm still figuring out what I want?
Definitely. Lots of people I work with come to me because they're not really sure what they want. We can figure it out together, and that can be a goal on its own.
