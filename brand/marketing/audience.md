# Ideal audience — Anna Hellmuth

## Primary audience

Highly educated professionals, creatives, and entrepreneurs.

People who are already functioning relatively well externally but feel stuck internally.

### Public-facing label

On the website and in marketing materials, use:

**creative & ambitious souls**

This is the warm, inclusive tagline. The profile above describes who you are actually trying to reach underneath that label.

### Website challenge themes (recognition copy)

Counseling audience signals from site content:

- Burnout and emotional exhaustion
- Anxiety and inner turmoil
- Emigration, displacement, identity in transition
- Relationship pain and attachment wounds
- Highly sensitive temperament (thin-skinned, people-pleasing)
- Career misalignment despite external success

Coaching audience signals:

- Dreams that feel impossible, scary, or out of reach
- Moving past fear toward business or creative success
- Finding authentic voice and visibility
- Clarity during major life or career change
- Redefining life's story after outgrowing old goals

## Counseling vs coaching

Help prospects self-select with outcomes, not jargon.

| | **Psychological counseling** | **Life coaching** |
|---|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Focus** | Healing, emotional work, processing the past | Goals, structure, future direction |
| **Best for** | Emotional burdens, trauma, relationships, identity, burnout | Ambitious goals, transitions, performance, life redesign |
| **Site tagline** | Find your power to release emotional burdens and heal your soul | Realize your ambitious goals and ascend to new heights |
| **Decision question** | Are my issues emotional or past-focused? | Are my issues goal or future-focused? |

When unclear, the free 30-minute discovery call helps determine fit. Clarity itself can be the goal.
