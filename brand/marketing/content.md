# Content guidelines — Anna Hellmuth

## Content philosophy

The goal is not to sound smart.

The goal is to make people feel:

*"She understands exactly what I experience."*

When people feel understood, trust develops naturally.

---

## Personal content lens

### Speak

- From experience, not from above
- As an equal, not as an authority figure
- Professionally but warmly
- Honestly without oversharing
- With conviction without preaching

### Avoid

- Sounding detached
- Sounding overly academic
- Sounding like a textbook
- Generic self-help advice
- Excessive caution that removes personality

---

## Content creation checklist

Before posting ask:

### Relevance

- Does this describe a real human experience?
- Would somebody recognize themselves in this?

### Specificity

- Is this concrete enough?
- Could somebody immediately think: "That's me"?

### Authenticity

- Do I genuinely believe this?
- Have I seen this in myself or clients?

### Emotional connection

- Does this make the reader feel seen?
- Does it create an emotional reaction?

### Depth

- Does this go beyond common advice?
- Does it offer a new perspective?

### Business value

- Does this subtly demonstrate how I think?
- Would an ideal client become more likely to work with me after reading this?

## Strong post formula

Posts perform best when they contain:

1. A recognizable experience
2. A surprising insight
3. An emotional truth
4. A practical implication

**Formula:**

You think X.  
Actually, Y is happening.  
That is why Z feels so difficult.

### Example skeleton (coaching)

> You think you're procrastinating because you're lazy.  
> Actually, part of you is afraid that succeeding will change how people see you.  
> That is why starting feels heavier than the task itself.

### Example skeleton (counseling)

> You think you should be "over it" by now.  
> Actually, your body is still holding what your mind tried to leave behind.  
> That is why certain situations still catch you off guard.

## Weekly content strategy

**Minimum:**

### Counseling — 1 post per week

Topics:

- Emotional regulation
- Shame
- Self-worth
- Attachment
- Boundaries
- Childhood conditioning
- Internal conflicts

### Coaching — 1 post per week

Topics:

- Fear and action
- Purpose
- Identity change
- Life transitions
- Authentic success
- Growth obstacles
- Self-leadership

Consistency matters more than volume.

---

## Content sources (in priority order)

1. Things you have genuinely struggled with
2. Things you have genuinely learned
3. Patterns you have observed repeatedly in clients (anonymized, no identifying details)
4. Ideas you genuinely believe

Do not default to generic educational content or modality explanations (CBT, hypnotherapy, etc.) in social posts.

---

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Name the inner experience before the solution | Lead with your credentials or method list |
| Use "you" and direct address | Sound clinical or cold |
| Offer one clear insight per post | Pack in multiple unrelated tips |
| End with possibility, not pressure | Use guilt, urgency, or "you must" language |
| Show how you think | Explain the full therapeutic process |
| Reference counseling or coaching lane when relevant | Blur the two services without clarity |

### Before / after

**Weak:** "As a medical doctor and psychologist, I use integrative methods including CBT and hypnotherapy to help clients achieve better mental health."

**Strong:** "You can explain your patterns perfectly and still feel stuck. Understanding isn't the same as release. That's often when people realize they need support going deeper than insight alone."
