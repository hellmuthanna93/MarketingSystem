## Testimonial themes to echo

When writing content, mirror what clients actually experience:

| Theme | Example signal |
|-------|----------------|
| Pattern awareness | "Became aware of my negative patterns … learned to regulate myself better" |
| Crisis relief | "From the very first session, I experienced a sense of relief" |
| Tailored approach | "You explained things in a way that finally made sense to my rational mind" |
| Transformation speed | "Accelerate my healing process … everything in my life is better than I expected" |
| Childhood / deep work | "Learned to process emotions constructively instead of suppressing them" |
| Warmth & safety | "I felt heard, understood, in a non-judgmental space" |
| Life overhaul | "Divorce, career pivot, finances, relationship — I learned to trust myself" |

Always attribute testimonials as *— Former client* with privacy note when published.

## Words & phrases to prefer

- transformation, inner change, clarity, relief, alignment
- courageous, brave (coaching context), authentic
- patterns, stuck, roadblocks, stepping stones
- heal, release, fulfill, ascend
- discovery call, resonate, mutual fit
- creative & ambitious souls

---

## Words & phrases to avoid

- guaranteed results, miracle, fix you
- hustle, grind, 10x (misaligned with calm feminine brand)
- clinical coldness: patient, disorder-first framing in marketing
- Generic self-help: "just think positive," "manifest your dreams"
- Pressure: "limited spots," "act now," "don't miss out"
- Over-academic openings in social content: "Research shows," "As a physician…"

---

## Multilingual

- **English** is primary for marketing system defaults
- DE / UK / RU: same visual brand and strategic principles; adapt idioms, not just translate
- Test Cyrillic and longer German compounds in social templates before publishing
- Keep CTAs consistent: discovery call / Beratungsgespräch / etc. (translate meaning, not word-for-word awkwardness)
