# Positioning - Anna Hellmuth

**Practice:** Psychological counseling & life coaching  
**Site:** [annahellmuth.com](https://annahellmuth.com/)

## Business vision

Build a highly profitable online coaching and psychological counseling practice that provides freedom, depth, meaningful work, and a sustainable lifestyle.

### Long-term goal (5 years)

- €300,000 annual income
- Maximum 6-7 working hours per day
- Two completely free days per week
- High-quality clients rather than high volume
- A calm, feminine, enjoyable lifestyle with time for reading, culture, relationships, dancing, and personal growth

### 2026 priorities

1. Consistent content creation
2. Growing audience trust
3. Increasing coaching clients
4. Refining positioning
5. Building systems that support work-life balance
6. Maintaining personal wellbeing while growing revenue

---

## Core positioning

You are not selling information.

You are helping people create lasting inner change.

Your strength is the combination of:

- Psychological depth
- Coaching practicality
- Emotional intelligence
- Ability to see underlying patterns
- Ability to explain complex inner processes clearly
- Genuine warmth and humanity

Many coaches focus only on motivation.  
Many therapists focus only on understanding.

You help people understand themselves and move forward.

---

## Core marketing principle

People do not buy coaching.

People buy movement.

They buy:

- Relief
- Hope
- Clarity
- Confidence
- Possibility
- Transformation

Always communicate the change.

Not the process.

Not the method.

Not the session itself.

---

## Guiding principle

The goal is not to become the loudest expert.

The goal is to become the person whose content makes the right clients think:

*"Finally someone understands what I have been experiencing."*

That feeling creates trust.  
Trust creates clients.

Your lived experience.

The strongest content comes from:

- Things you have genuinely struggled with
- Things you have genuinely learned
