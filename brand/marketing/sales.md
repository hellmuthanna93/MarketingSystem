# Sales & client attraction - Anna Hellmuth

## Sales philosophy

Do not convince.  
Do not push.  
Do not pressure.

Instead:

- Help people understand their situation
- Show a possible future
- Explain what keeps them stuck
- Invite them to take the next step

People should feel respected and free.

Website-aligned invitation: *"Why not give it a try and find out!"*

---

## Pricing mindset

Your work creates meaningful change.

- Do not apologize for pricing
- Do not justify pricing excessively
- Do not assume people cannot afford it
- Allow adults to make their own decisions

---

## Client attraction strategy

Focus on:

### Trust

People buy from people they trust.

### Visibility

People cannot hire someone they never see.

### Consistency

Small actions repeated over time outperform occasional bursts of activity.

### Depth

One powerful post can create more clients than twenty generic posts.

## Discovery flow

1. **Free 30-minute intake call** - not therapy; mutual fit check
2. Ask questions, check resonance
3. Begin working together only if both agree

What the intake offers:

- Get a sense of who Anna is and whether she's the right specialist
- Clarify needs and whether counseling or coaching fits
- Discuss practical questions (format, frequency, pricing)
