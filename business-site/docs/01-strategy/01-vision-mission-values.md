# Vision, Mission & Values

## Vision

A highly profitable online counseling and coaching practice that provides freedom, depth, meaningful work, and a sustainable, feminine lifestyle - proof that you can do psychologically serious work without grinding yourself down.

**Five-year picture:** €300,000 annual income, ≤6-7 working hours/day, two completely free days per week, a roster of high-quality clients rather than high volume, and time for reading, culture, relationships, dancing, and personal growth.

## Mission

To help sensitive, thoughtful people create the lasting inner change that understanding alone could never give them - combining medical and psychological depth with coaching practicality and genuine warmth.

From the about page: *"I empower individuals to achieve greater well-being through transformative journeys, integrating medical and psychological expertise."*

## Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **Depth** | We go beneath symptoms and advice to the pattern underneath. No quick fixes, no surface motivation. |
| **Warmth with backbone** | Real compassion and steady, grounded certainty at once. Never pity, never coldness. |
| **Lived honesty** | We speak from experience, as an equal - including our own burnout and pivots. No posturing. |
| **Respect for the client** | Clients are intelligent adults who hold complexity and make their own decisions. No pressure, no infantilizing. |
| **Sustainability** | The practice serves the life, not the other way around. Work-life balance is a design constraint, not an afterthought. |
| **Integrity of claims** | We never promise outcomes we cannot guarantee, never invent proof, and protect every client's privacy. |

## Why it matters

These values are not decoration. They shape pricing (no apology, no discount-chasing), marketing (movement over hype), and operations (protected free days). When a decision conflicts with a value, the value wins.
