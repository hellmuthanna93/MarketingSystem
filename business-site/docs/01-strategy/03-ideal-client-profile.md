# Ideal Client Profile

> Source of truth for audience: [brand/marketing/audience.md](../../../brand/marketing/audience.md). This doc condenses it into segments, triggers, and an anti-ICP for strategic use.

## Public-facing label

In marketing, use **sensitive, thoughtful souls** — the warm, inclusive tagline. The profiles below describe who that label actually reaches.

*(Legacy live-site hero still says "creative & ambitious souls"; prefer the label above in new content.)*

## The shared core

Sensitive, deep-feeling people who understand a lot and genuinely strive for change — often highly educated professionals who function well externally but feel stuck internally. They have read, reflected, and analyzed. What they need is guided support to move through what understanding alone cannot shift.

## Segment A — Coaching client

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Men and women, 30–45; skilled professionals or thoughtful seekers |
| Temperament | Goal-oriented doers; act and produce, but not very aware of their emotions; often the oldest child |
| Context | High-functioning, craving depth; often living abroad or in transition |
| Inner experience | Something feels *off* despite stability; longing vs fear; loses contact with self while chasing goals; forces forward, burns out |
| Variant | Arrives already exhausted — ready for change but without the energy to make it alone |
| Struggles | Paralysis, procrastination, emotional avoidance, fear of failure/success, indecision, identity confusion, blocked creativity, loneliness |

## Segment B — Counseling client

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Typically a woman 25–45; also gay men; highly trained, sensitive professional in a demanding field |
| Origin | Often cold, emotionally unavailable, or absent parents; a deep hole inside |
| Background | Ukrainian women abroad (one meaningful segment); other immigration histories; locals with the same patterns |
| Inner experience | Suppressed emotions, neglected needs; mild-to-moderate depression, anxiety, overthinking; harsh self-demand; hidden shame; lost goals and orientation |
| Relationships | Insecure attachment — fear of losing partner, or inability to form a deep bond |
| Body image | Often dislikes own body, carries shame, while presenting carefully |
| Struggles | Depression, anxiety, perfectionism, shame, attachment fear, body image, exhaustion, busyness/scrolling to avoid feeling |

## Triggers (when they reach out)

- A burnout wall — still delivering, but empty inside
- A relationship rupture or fear of one
- A major transition: move, career pivot, divorce, emigration
- A "quiet Sunday dread" moment — realizing success doesn't feel like anything
- Repeated failure to change despite knowing exactly what's wrong

## What they want

Authentic self-expression, confidence, meaningful success, alignment of values and life, courage for important goals, growth, life redesign, better relationships, emotional freedom.

## Anti-ICP (who this is NOT for)

- People seeking quick fixes without willingness to do inner work
- Those wanting motivation-only coaching without depth
- Anyone demanding guaranteed outcomes
- People looking for clinical Psychotherapie / acute crisis treatment (refer out — see [../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md](../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md))

## Languages & reach

Online, location-independent. Sessions in **English, German, Ukrainian, Russian**, or a mix. English is the marketing default.

## So what

Two lanes, one inner pattern (insight-rich but stuck). Marketing speaks to the pattern and lets people self-select by lane. This feeds messaging ([../02-brand/05-messaging.md](../02-brand/05-messaging.md)) and the content engine ([../04-marketing/02-content-engine.md](../04-marketing/02-content-engine.md)).
