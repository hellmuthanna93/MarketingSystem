# SWOT Analysis

## Strengths

- Rare credential combination: Medical Doctor + psychologist + coach + hypnotherapist (ongoing)
- Genuine psychological **depth** paired with coaching **practicality**
- Lived experience (surgery → burnout → psychology) — authentic, non-performative authority
- Multilingual: EN / DE / UK / RU with real migration experience and cultural fit
- Precise, warm voice that makes the right client feel instantly understood
- Location-independent, low-overhead online model
- Clear premium positioning and refusal to commoditize

## Weaknesses

- Solo capacity ceiling — depth model limits sessions/day
- Inbound/reputation funnel is slow to compound; little paid acceleration
- Single-founder dependency (all delivery + marketing on Anna)
- Wedge offer (Clarity Session) not yet validated or live
- Newsletter / owned audience not yet established (TBD)
- No systematic referral engine yet
- Pricing/legal scope (Beratung vs Psychotherapie) needs tight discipline

## Opportunities

- Large underserved expat market in DACH, especially Ukrainian women abroad
- Rising "successful but empty" / burnout awareness among high-functioning professionals
- Productized wedge (Clarity Session) to convert audience without full-package commitment
- Content compounding into authority across IG / LI / blog / SEO
- Digital products / lead magnets for leverage beyond 1:1 hours
- Multilingual content as a near-uncontested niche

## Threats

- Crowded, low-trust coaching market and algorithm dependence
- Regulatory limits on health advertising and scope (HWG, Heilkunde)
- Price competition from app-based therapy (perception, not direct)
- Burnout risk if growth chases volume instead of price/packaging
- Economic downturn reducing discretionary spend on premium self-pay services

## TOWS — cross strategies

| | Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---------------|---------|
| **Strengths** | **SO:** Use the rare combination + multilingual depth to own the expat/insight-rich niche with productized wedge and compounding content | **ST:** Lean on lived authority + compliance discipline to stand out in a noisy, regulated market without hype |
| **Weaknesses** | **WO:** Launch the Clarity Session and a newsletter to build owned audience and leverage beyond 1:1 hours | **WT:** Protect against burnout and capacity limits by raising price/packaging and building referral + content systems, not volume |

## So what

The dominant move is **SO**: productize the wedge, build owned audience, and let multilingual depth content compound — while **WT** discipline (price up, systematize, protect free days) keeps the practice sustainable.
