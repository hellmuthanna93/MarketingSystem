# Business Model & Revenue Streams

## How the practice makes money

A premium, self-pay, **1:1 online** model with a productized entry offer and multi-session packages — designed for high revenue per client at low volume, in line with the work-life-balance north star.

## Revenue streams

| Stream | Type | Status | Role |
|--------|------|--------|------|
| Counseling sessions & 6-pack | 1:1 recurring | Live | Core healing-lane revenue |
| Coaching sessions & packages (6 / 12) | 1:1 recurring | Live | Highest per-client value |
| **Clarity Session wedge** | 1:1 one-off | **Proposed / TBD** | Low-commitment entry that converts to packages |
| Digital products / lead magnets | Leverage | Future | Audience capture + scalable income |
| Newsletter (owned audience) | Leverage | Future / TBD | Nurture + conversion engine |

## Engagement types

1. **Discovery call (free, 30 min)** — fit check, lane decision, recommendation.
2. **Clarity Session (proposed wedge)** — single 90-min session + written summary; bridge to packages.
3. **Single session** — one-off support for a specific issue.
4. **Package** — 6 or 12 sessions; the depth work where transformation actually happens; the revenue backbone.

## Leverage model

Anna's time is the constraint. Leverage comes from, in priority order:

1. **Price & packaging** — premium positioning, package bias over singles (better outcomes *and* economics).
2. **Wedge conversion** — productized entry that turns interest into committed packages efficiently.
3. **Content compounding** — one strong post or blog works for months; reputation lowers acquisition cost over time.
4. **Productization** — eventual digital products / lead magnets that earn outside session hours.

Explicitly **not** leverage here: more hours, lower prices, or volume discounting. Those break the north star.

- Coaching package (12) at €1,920 = the flagship; a handful per month moves the needle far more than many single sessions.
- The wedge (€250–€300, TBD) prices a premium one-off that both stands alone and credits psychologically toward a package decision.

## So what

The model wins on **revenue per client**, not client count. Strategy, marketing, and operations all bend toward fewer, deeper, better-fit clients who buy packages — with the wedge as the conversion bridge. Next: [07-pricing-and-packaging.md](07-pricing-and-packaging.md).
