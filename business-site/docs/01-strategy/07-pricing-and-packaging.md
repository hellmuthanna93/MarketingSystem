## Packaging logic

- **Counseling keeps singles and a package.**
- **Coaching has one complete program.** No coaching singles or alternate
  packages.
- **The wedge may fill the gap.** The proposed Clarity Session can let a
  prospect experience the depth before committing.

## Price ladder (ascending)

```
Free discovery call → counseling single (€120) / counseling 6-pack (€660)
   → proposed Clarity Session (€250-€300, TBD) → The Next Chapter (€1,950)
```

## Pricing mindset (from sales.md)

- Do not apologize for pricing.
- Do not over-justify it.
- Do not assume people cannot afford it.
- Allow adults to make their own decisions.

The work creates meaningful change; the price reflects that. No "limited spots", no discount pressure, no guilt.

## So what

Premium, package-biased pricing supports the €300k-at-low-volume goal. The wedge is the missing rung that should lift conversion from interest to commitment. Modeled financially in [../07-finance-legal/02-finance-model.md](../07-finance-legal/02-finance-model.md).
