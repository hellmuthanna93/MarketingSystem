# Business Plan

A concise formal plan for the practice. Figures are targets and assumptions, not guarantees; financial detail lives in [../07-finance-legal/](../07-finance-legal/).

## 1. Executive summary

Anna Hellmuth runs a premium online psychological counseling and life coaching practice serving sensitive, high-functioning, often international clients in EN / DE / UK / RU. The differentiator is a rare blend of medical and psychological depth with coaching practicality and lived experience. The plan: grow inbound reputation, launch a productized Clarity Session wedge, and convert depth-led trust into multi-session packages — reaching €300k/year at low volume while protecting a sustainable lifestyle.

## 2. Practice & offering

Online 1:1 counseling and coaching; free discovery call → (proposed) Clarity Session → single sessions → 6/12-session packages. Full catalog: [../03-offerings/01-service-catalog.md](../03-offerings/01-service-catalog.md).

## 3. Market

Online DACH + international/expat market; large underserved multilingual segment (incl. Ukrainian women abroad); rising burnout awareness among professionals. Analysis: [04-market-and-competitors.md](04-market-and-competitors.md).

## 4. Positioning & differentiation

"You understand yourself and you're still stuck — I'm the bridge." Depth + forward movement + warmth + lived experience + multilingual fit. Detail: [02-positioning-and-niche.md](02-positioning-and-niche.md).

## 5. Ideal clients

Two lanes — counseling (past/emotional) and coaching (future/goals) — sharing one inner pattern: insight-rich but stuck. Detail: [03-ideal-client-profile.md](03-ideal-client-profile.md).

## 6. Go-to-market

Inbound, reputation-led. Consistent depth content on Instagram (primary), LinkedIn (authority), with blog/SEO and an owned newsletter (TBD). Warm/referral outreach only (UWG-compliant). Funnel: awareness → follow → discovery call → wedge/package. Detail: [../04-marketing/](../04-marketing/).

## 7. Sales

No-pressure consultative flow: discovery call → recommendation → wedge or package. Detail: [../05-sales/](../05-sales/).

## 8. Operations

Lean online stack (calendar, video, payment, notes, content tools), standardized session SOPs, and a clear quality bar. Protected free days are an operating constraint. Detail: [../06-operations/](../06-operations/).

## 9. Financials (targets)

- **Near-term:** stabilize consistent client flow; validate the wedge.
- **5-year target:** €300,000/year at ≤6–7 hrs/day, 2 free days/week.
- Revenue driven by package mix and price, not session volume. Scenarios: [../07-finance-legal/05-financial-projections.md](../07-finance-legal/05-financial-projections.md).

## 10. Legal & risk

German setup, Beratung vs Psychotherapie scope, HWG advertising limits, GDPR for health data, professional liability. Detail: [../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md](../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md). Key risks and mitigations: [05-swot.md](05-swot.md).

## 11. Roadmap

90-day launch → transition milestones → KPIs → master backlog. Detail: [../08-roadmap/](../08-roadmap/).

## So what

The plan is coherent and low-capital: the scarce resource is Anna's time and trust, so every lever (price, wedge, content, packaging) protects depth and freedom. Execution starts at [../08-roadmap/01-90-day-launch.md](../08-roadmap/01-90-day-launch.md).
