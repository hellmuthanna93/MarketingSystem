# Naming

## Decision

The practice operates under the founder's own name: **Anna Hellmuth - Psychological Counseling and Life Coaching**. This is settled; no rebrand is planned.

## Rationale

- **Trust is personal.** In counseling and coaching, clients hire a person, not a brand. The founder's name is the most credible, least abstract label.
- **Credentials attach to the name.** "Anna Hellmuth, MD" carries the medical + psychological authority directly.
- **Multilingual neutrality.** A personal name travels cleanly across EN / DE / UK / RU without translation or connotation problems.
- **Domain & presence already established.** annahellmuth.com, @anna_hellmuth.md, and directory listings are consistent.
