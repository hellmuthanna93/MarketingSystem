# Voice & Tone

> **Pointer doc.** The full voice guide is [brand/voice.md](../../../brand/voice.md). The agent-enforced version is the `practice-voice` skill (`.cursor/skills/practice-voice/`) with `humanizer-patterns.md`. Read those before writing any copy.

## Voice in one line

Warm and understanding, **and** unmistakably confident that inner states can change - a specialist who names the reader's experience precisely, has been there too, and stays grounded as the one who can help them move.

## Core attributes

- Felt inner strength and compassion (firmness + care)
- Not separate from the struggle - known from the inside
- Precise recognition - *"this is written about me"*
- Grounded certainty, not hype
- Respects intelligent adults
- Honest about limits

## The signature move

Lead with recognition, then confidence, then the way through:

> You think X. Actually, Y is happening. That is why Z feels so difficult.

## Tone shifts by channel

| Channel | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Instagram | Tight, intimate, one felt insight; conversational |
| LinkedIn | Same depth, slightly more room to develop the insight; authority without posturing |
| Blog | Flowing paragraphs, movement-first opening, credentials in bio not hook |
| Email | Brief, warm, human moment before logistics |
| Web | Confident, calm, sentence/title case; not shouty |
| Wedge deliverable | Precise, warm, ends on possibility + clear next step |

## Never-list (summary)

Motivational slogans · downplaying · pity/helplessness · making yourself small · parent-to-child voice · coldness or showing off · trying to impress · pressure/urgency · over-academic openings.

Full never-list and quick-check: `practice-voice` skill.

## So what

Voice is the brand's heaviest-lifting asset - it's what makes the right client feel understood. Keep it identical in spirit across channels; only the length and intimacy shift. Evidence from real writing: [07-voice-reference.md](07-voice-reference.md).
