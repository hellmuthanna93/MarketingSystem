# Case Study Strategy

> Confidentiality is non-negotiable. In a counseling context, proof is built from **patterns, never people**. Operational rules: `case-studies-anonymized` skill.

## The principle

Tell the pattern, not the person. Composite, generalized stories that many clients recognize - never an identifiable individual. This protects clients, stays within HWG/GDPR limits, and is actually *more* persuasive, because the reader sees themselves in the universality.

## What we can tell

- Composite patterns ("Many high-functioning clients arrive saying…")
- Anonymous, opt-in testimonials (attribute *- Former client*, with privacy note)
- Theme-led stories drawn from the testimonial themes in [brand/marketing/messaging.md](../../../brand/marketing/messaging.md)

## What we never tell

- Names, ages, nationalities, professions, locations, or any identifying detail
- Direct session quotes without explicit written consent
- Disease-cure claims tied to a story (HWG - see [../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md](../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md))
- Any detail combination that could make a real client recognizable

## Structure for a pattern story

1. **Recognizable arrival** - how this kind of client first describes the problem (composite)
2. **The pattern underneath** - the real insight
3. **What shifts with support** - the movement, honestly; no miracle, no guarantee
4. **Soft invitation** - optional discovery-call CTA

## Testimonial handling

- Always anonymous: *- Former client*
- Privacy note: *feedback from clients is posted anonymously to protect their privacy*
- Opt-in only; easy to decline (see testimonial-ask email in [../09-toolkit/05-client-emails.md](../09-toolkit/05-client-emails.md))
- About experience and process, not cure claims

## Which stories to prioritize

Stories that mirror the ICP's core triggers - burnout/success-empty, attachment fear, expat displacement, "insight wasn't enough", the breakthrough after stuckness - because they make the right client feel seen.

## So what

Proof is built ethically through pattern recognition, not exposure. This protects clients and the brand while still demonstrating that Anna understands and can help. Feeds content ([../04-marketing/02-content-engine.md](../04-marketing/02-content-engine.md)) and toolkit posts.
