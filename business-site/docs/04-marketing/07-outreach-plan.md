# Outreach Plan

> Warm and referral-first only. Cold outreach to individuals is off the table (UWG — see [06-outreach-compliance-germany.md](06-outreach-compliance-germany.md)). This plan is light by design — Anna's growth is inbound-led.

## Principle

Outreach for this practice = nurturing warm relationships and referral partnerships, not prospecting strangers. It complements content; it doesn't replace it.

## Warm sources (in priority order)

1. **People who already engage** — respond to comments and DMs warmly; invite a discovery call only when natural.
2. **Past clients** — gentle check-ins; renewal invitations (see [../09-toolkit/05-client-emails.md](../09-toolkit/05-client-emails.md)).
3. **Referral partners** — peers/professionals who serve the same ICP (e.g. GPs, expat services, other coaches who refer out for depth work, German therapists with waitlists who refer self-pay/expats).
4. **Communities** — expat groups, professional networks where Anna is a genuine member (contribute, don't pitch).

## Referral partnership approach

- Identify 5–10 professionals whose clients overlap Anna's ICP but who don't offer what she does.
- Build genuine relationships; offer reciprocity (refer back when appropriate).
- Make it easy: a one-line description of who she helps + the discovery-call link.
- No commissions that could create conflicts in a counseling context; keep it ethical.

## Volume targets (gentle)

This is relationship work, not a quota. A reasonable rhythm:

- Respond to all warm inbound within 24–48h
- Nurture 1–2 referral relationships per month
- Quarterly: a warm check-in with past clients who opted in

## Cadence for warm conversations

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    engage[Warm signal: comment / DM / referral] --> respond[Respond warmly, no pitch]
    respond --> listen[Understand their situation]
    listen --> invite["Invite discovery call (if fit)"]
    invite --> book[Book]
```

## Never

- Cold email / cold call / cold DM as advertising
- Pressure, urgency, or guilt
- Mass templated outreach

## So what

Outreach is a small, ethical, relationship-based supplement to inbound — warm responses, referral partners, and opted-in past clients. The heavy lifting stays with content and the discovery-call funnel.
