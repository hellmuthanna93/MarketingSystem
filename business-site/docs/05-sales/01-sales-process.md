# Sales Process

> Sales here means a respectful, consultative conversation — never persuasion or pressure. Philosophy: do not convince, do not push. Help people understand their situation, show a possible future, name what keeps them stuck, invite the next step. People should feel respected and free.

## Pipeline stages

| Stage | What happens | Exit |
|-------|--------------|------|
| 1. Inbound | Person engages with content / referral | They click the discovery-call link |
| 2. Discovery call (free, 30 min) | Understand, name the pattern, recommend | Recommendation made |
| 3. Recommendation | Wedge or package or single, by fit | They consider |
| 4. Decision | They decide in their own time | Booked or not (both fine) |
| 5. Onboarding | Intake, access, first session | Engagement begins |
| 6. Delivery | Sessions; quality bar | Progress |
| 7. Renewal / referral | Continue or refer | Compounding |

## Qualification (during discovery)

Use the listen-fors from the `discovery-calls` skill:

- **Counseling signals:** depression, anxiety, shame, attachment fear, body image, burnout, "understanding hasn't been enough"
- **Coaching signals:** stuck on goals, fear/paralysis, identity change, transition
- **Not a fit:** wants guarantees, quick fixes without inner work, acute clinical crisis (refer out)

## Timing

- Respond to warm inbound within 24–48h
- Discovery call: 30 minutes, free
- After the call: send a short follow-up with the recommendation + link (same day or next)
- Wedge → package: day-7 follow-up email
- No pressure to decide on the call

## The recommendation logic

| Situation | Recommend |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clear, committed, knows the work | 6-pack (counseling) or coaching package |
| Wants to feel the depth first | Clarity Session wedge (TBD) |
| Single specific issue | Single session |
| Acute clinical / out of scope | Refer out honestly |

## Pricing conversations

No apology, no over-justification, no assuming they can't afford it. State the price plainly and let the adult decide. (See [../01-strategy/07-pricing-and-packaging.md](../01-strategy/07-pricing-and-packaging.md).)

## So what

The "sale" is really a clear, kind recommendation after genuinely understanding the person. Trust does the converting.
