## SOP authoring rules

- One purpose per SOP; numbered steps.
- Reference the template, don't duplicate it.
- Keep them short enough to actually follow.
- Update the SOP (and `practice-profile`) when facts change.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- **Mon:** plan week; confirm sessions; batch content
- **Tue-Thu:** sessions + delivery (within hour limit)
- **Fri:** notes, summaries, follow-ups, admin/invoicing; weekly review
- **2 days:** free (protected)

## So what

SOPs turn the strategy into muscle memory. The index is the operations map; templates make each SOP one-click repeatable. Quality is governed by [04-quality-bar.md](04-quality-bar.md).
