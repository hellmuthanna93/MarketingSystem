# Finance Model

> Illustrative model showing how the €300k goal is reachable at low volume. Figures use current prices; treat as planning scenarios, not guarantees. Confirm tax treatment with a Steuerberater ([01-legal-setup-germany.md](01-legal-setup-germany.md)).

## The constraint

Anna's time is the scarce resource: ≤6-7 working hours/day, 2 free days/week. So revenue must come from **value per client**, not client count. The model is built around package mix and price, not session volume.

## Capacity frame

Assume ~3 working days dedicated to sessions (rest for content, delivery prep, admin), well within the hour limit. At a sustainable depth pace, that supports a limited number of session-hours/week - which is exactly why **price/mix** must do the heavy lifting.

## Illustrative path to €300k/year

€300k is approximately **€25k/month**. The previous model depended on retired
coaching packages and is no longer valid. Rebuild the scenario after the new
program price is confirmed and the first paid pilots reveal the true delivery
time.

## Why the wedge matters financially

The Clarity Session (€250-€300) monetizes prospects who aren't ready for a package and converts a share of them into packages - raising average revenue per lead without adding raw volume.

## Cost structure (lean)

| Cost | Nature |
|------|--------|
| Tools (video, payment, notes, newsletter, forms) | Low monthly |
| Insurance (Berufshaftpflicht) | Annual |
| Steuerberater / legal | Periodic |
| Website / hosting | Low |
| Optional: assistant / paid promotion | Future |
| Taxes (income; USt where applicable) | Per [01-legal-setup-germany.md](01-legal-setup-germany.md) |

Low overhead means a high share of revenue is margin - the model's strength.

## Levers (in priority order)

1. **Price** - raise rates as demand/reputation grow
2. **Mix** - increase the share of well-fit coaching-program clients
3. **Wedge conversion** - more leads → packages
4. **Consistency** - predictable pipeline reduces feast/famine
5. **Leverage (later)** - products that earn outside session hours

## So what

€300k at low volume is feasible **only** via premium pricing + package mix + wedge conversion - never via more hours. The model says: protect the hours, raise the value. Scenarios over time: [05-financial-projections.md](05-financial-projections.md).
