# Savings & Runway

> A solo practice has variable income. A cash cushion turns that volatility from a stressor into a non-issue — and protects the no-pressure brand (you sell better when you don't need the sale).

## Why a cushion matters here

- Income fluctuates month to month (packages sold unevenly).
- The brand depends on **not** being pushy; financial pressure erodes that.
- The work-life-balance goal requires the freedom to say no to bad-fit clients.

## Target runway

| Tier | Cushion | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| Minimum | 3 months of personal + business costs | Absorbs a slow quarter |
| Comfortable | 6 months | Decisions from security, not fear |
| Strong | 12 months | Full freedom to be selective and invest in leverage |

**Action:** calculate monthly personal + business burn, set the target tier, and hold the cushion in an accessible account separate from operating cash.

## Smoothing variable income

- **Separate accounts:** operating, taxes (set aside USt/income tax as earned — see [01-legal-setup-germany.md](01-legal-setup-germany.md)), and personal pay.
- **Owner's draw:** pay yourself a steady monthly amount from the operating account, not whatever happened to land.
- **Tax reserve:** ring-fence a percentage of every payment so tax time is a non-event.

## Triggers (when the cushion frees a move)

- Cushion at "comfortable" → invest in leverage (lead magnet, newsletter, possibly help).
- Cushion at "strong" → raise prices and be more selective without anxiety.
- Cushion below "minimum" → tighten costs, focus on pipeline; do **not** discount or chase volume in panic.

## So what

A 3–6+ month cushion plus disciplined account separation makes income volatility irrelevant and keeps the brand calm and non-pushy. Set the number, automate the reserves. Projections that inform the target: [05-financial-projections.md](05-financial-projections.md).
