# Growth Milestones (toward the 5-year goal)

> The original template framed this as "transition to full-time." Anna's practice is already her work (online since 2023), so this doc reframes it as **growth gates** on the path to the €300k / low-volume / 2-free-days north star.

## The destination

- €300,000 annual income
- ≤6-7 working hours/day
- 2 completely free days/week
- High-quality clients over volume

## Gates

### Gate 1 - Consistency (0-6 months)

**Triggers to consider you've passed:**

- 12+ weeks of uninterrupted 1+1 weekly content
- Discovery calls arriving weekly from inbound
- Wedge validated (sold + delivered + some conversion to packages)

**Then:** raise the floor - formalize systems, hold the rhythm.

### Gate 2 - Predictable pipeline (6-12 months)

**Triggers:**

- Predictable monthly discovery calls and package sales
- A growing owned audience (newsletter subscribers)
- Referral relationships producing occasional warm leads
- Revenue trending toward a stable monthly baseline

**Then:** raise prices and/or shift mix toward higher-value coaching packages; consider a first digital product.

### Gate 3 - Leverage (12+ months)

**Triggers:**

- Capacity pressure (good problem) - calendar filling within the work-hour limit
- Strong content compounding; lower effort per lead

**Then:** add leverage that protects free days - digital products, selective price increases, possibly a waitlist - **never** more hours or volume discounting.

## Financial gates (guardrails)

- Don't add capacity by extending hours past the limit; add it by **price/packaging**.
- Maintain a runway cushion (see [../07-finance-legal/03-savings-and-cushion.md](../07-finance-legal/03-savings-and-cushion.md)).
- Reinvest in systems and leverage, not vanity spend.

## So what

Growth is gated on consistency → predictability → leverage, and each gate is passed by raising value, not hours. This keeps the path to €300k aligned with the freedom goal. Measured by [03-milestones-and-kpis.md](03-milestones-and-kpis.md).
