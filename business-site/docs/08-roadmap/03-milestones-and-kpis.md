# Milestones & KPIs

Measurable success criteria by phase. Keep the metric set small — depth practices die from vanity-metric chasing.

## Guiding metric

**Discovery calls booked per month** from inbound. It captures the whole funnel's health (reach → resonance → action) in one number. Everything upstream serves it; everything downstream depends on it.

## KPI set

| Layer | Metric | Why it matters |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Content | Posts published / week (target: ≥2, 1 counseling + 1 coaching) | Consistency is the engine |
| Content | "That's me" signals (saves, resonant DMs/comments) | Resonance > raw reach |
| Audience | Newsletter subscribers (once live) | Owned, algorithm-resilient |
| Funnel | **Discovery calls booked / month** | The guiding metric |
| Funnel | Discovery → wedge/package conversion rate | Recommendation quality |
| Offer | Wedge → package conversion rate | Wedge validation |
| Revenue | Monthly revenue; package mix | Toward €300k via value not volume |
| Wellbeing | Working hours/day; free days kept | The north star guardrail |

## Milestones by horizon

### Day 90

- [ ] 12 consecutive weeks of 1+1 weekly posts
- [ ] Wedge confirmed, sold, delivered
- [ ] Discovery calls weekly from inbound
- [ ] Newsletter signup live
- [ ] Free days protected

### Month 6 (Gate 1)

- [ ] Predictable weekly discovery calls
- [ ] Wedge → package conversion observable
- [ ] Growing newsletter list
- [ ] Stable monthly revenue baseline

### Month 12 (Gate 2)

- [ ] Predictable monthly package sales
- [ ] Referral relationships producing occasional leads
- [ ] First price increase or mix shift toward coaching packages
- [ ] Capacity used efficiently within the work-hour limit

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** posts shipped? calls booked?
- **Monthly:** funnel conversion, revenue, hours/free days
- **Quarterly:** milestone check; adjust strategy

## So what

Track few things, honestly: consistency in, discovery calls and conversions through, revenue and wellbeing out. If the guiding metric (calls/month) is healthy and free days are intact, the practice is on track. Tasks: [04-task-backlog.md](04-task-backlog.md).
