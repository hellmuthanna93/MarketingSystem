# Business Growth Strategy

This document records Anna's long-term business-growth strategy. The natal-chart reflection is used as a personal and psychological lens; the actions below are grounded in the actual counseling and coaching business.

## North-star direction

Move from a business that relies mainly on one-to-one sessions toward:

**1:1 expertise -> own methodology -> teaching and content -> groups and workshops -> public professional authority.**

The immediate website migration remains the first priority. This strategy guides planning after that launch work is stable.

## Financial model and guardrails

- Aim toward €8,500 monthly revenue without trying to reach it through one-to-one sessions alone.
- Keep paid sessions at roughly 10-12 per week maximum.
- Keep psychological counseling at the current published level of about €120 per session for now.
- Sell coaching primarily as a clear 3-4 month package rather than isolated €180 sessions.
- Test a 12-session coaching package at €2,100-€2,400, with a concrete outcome.
- Do not add extensive unpaid WhatsApp support to justify package pricing.
- Decide in advance how many reduced-price counseling places are available, with a maximum of roughly 10-15% of client capacity.
- Do not offer spontaneous discounts or unpaid over-delivery.
- When income arrives, allocate predetermined percentages to taxes, business reserve, personal income, and long-term investing.

## Positioning

Public positioning should focus on:

> Psychological depth for sensitive, ambitious, high-functioning adults navigating identity, career, or life transitions.

This can be supported by Anna's medical background, psychology, hypnotherapy, multilingual work, and migration experience. Broader issues can remain part of private client work without being the main marketing message.

### Core niche and public message

Do not niche primarily around a diagnosis such as anxiety. The niche is transformation of identity and life direction:

> Psychological counseling and coaching for sensitive, ambitious, high-functioning adults who look successful on the outside but feel stuck, disconnected from themselves, or are going through a major life transition.

The primary public message is:

> For sensitive, ambitious people who have achieved a lot but feel stuck between who they have been and who they want to become - working with perfectionism, identity, relationships, and major life transitions.

Under this umbrella, offer psychological counseling for deeper emotional and attachment work, and coaching for career, self-realisation, and building the next stage of life.

### Areas to emphasise

- Identity and self-realisation: understanding who someone is and what kind of life they want.
- Perfectionism, overthinking, achievement pressure, and self-worth.
- Career, business, and life transitions: reinvention, leaving an old identity, starting something new, becoming more visible, and finding meaningful work.
- Attachment and relationship patterns: rejection wounds, unavailable partners, fear of intimacy, and fear of losing oneself.
- Migration, expat identity, and belonging between cultures as a distinctive secondary specialisation.

## Growth pillars

### 1. Higher-value coaching offer

- Package coaching around career transition, self-realisation, rebuilding life after relocation, or ambitious-but-stuck/high-functioning clients.
- Use clear outcomes and boundaries rather than extra unpaid availability.

### 2. Referral network

- Contact three potential referral partners each week.
- Target therapists who do not work in Anna's languages, physicians, coaches, expat professionals, relocation services, international communities, women's professional networks, and relevant consultants.
- Build toward 50 strong referral relationships within six months.

### 3. Workshops and B2B

- Create one excellent 90-minute workshop before creating several offers.
- Possible first topics: *Why We Love the Way We Love*, *Energy and Procrastination for High Achievers*, or *Migration and Identity*.
- Test it B2C at roughly €39-€69 per person.
- Then offer a refined version to companies and communities at roughly €600-€1,000.

### 4. Content with a business purpose

- Publish two strong pieces of content per week once the website migration is complete.
- At least one should have a direct call to action: discovery call, workshop, newsletter, or a specific service.
- Evaluate content by inquiries, discovery calls, and clients generated, not mainly by likes.

### 5. Discovery-call funnel

- Treat the free discovery call as a defined sales process as well as a helpful conversation.
- Track monthly: inquiries -> discovery calls -> offers made -> clients -> revenue.
- Initial reference point: 4-6 qualified discovery calls per month.
- Use the data to distinguish an acquisition problem from a positioning, offer, or sales-process problem.

## Weekly CEO priorities

After the website migration, complete these before website perfection, additional certifications, new courses, or endless branding refinement:

- [ ] Make three referral outreaches.
- [ ] Make one partnership or B2B pitch.
- [ ] Publish two purposeful content pieces.
- [ ] Follow up with every warm lead.
- [ ] Review revenue and leads once a week.

## When planning future work

Prioritise actions that directly strengthen referral relationships, coaching-package sales, workshops or B2B, discovery-call conversion, and financial systems. Keep one-to-one work sustainable rather than expanding it as the only route to growth.
