# Discovery Call Script

A 30-minute, no-pressure structure. The goal is not to sell - it's to understand, name the pattern, and offer a clear next step. Pairs with the `discovery-calls` skill and [../05-sales/02-discovery-to-wedge-script.md](../05-sales/02-discovery-to-wedge-script.md).

> Adapt wording to the person and language. Never read it robotically. Replace `{{placeholders}}`.

## Before the call

- [ ] Glance at any intake notes / how they found you
- [ ] Be rested and present - this call sets the tone
- [ ] Have the recommendation paths in mind (refer / single / wedge / package)

## 1. Welcome & frame (2 min)

> "Hi {{name}}, it's good to meet you. This is a free half hour just to understand what's going on for you and see whether working together would be a good fit. There's nothing you need to prepare, and no obligation at the end. Does that sound okay?"

## 2. What brings you here (8-10 min)

> "So - tell me what's been going on. What made you reach out now?"

Then listen. Don't fix yet. Follow-ups:

- "How long has this been with you?"
- "What does it feel like from the inside?"
- "What have you already tried?"
- "What would 'better' actually look like for you?"

Listen-fors (qualification):

- **Counseling:** depression, anxiety, shame, attachment fear, body image, burnout, "I understand it but can't shift it"
- **Coaching:** stuck on goals, fear/paralysis, identity change, transition, "I know what I want but can't act"
- **Out of scope:** acute crisis, wanting guarantees → refer out honestly

## 3. Name the pattern (5-7 min)

This is where trust is earned. Reflect back the inner conflict precisely:

> "What I'm hearing is - {{precise reflection of the pattern, e.g. 'you function beautifully on the outside, and inside there's this constant bracing, like rest isn't allowed'}}. Does that land?"

> "And it sounds like understanding *why* hasn't been enough to change it. That's actually really common - insight and release are two different things."

## 4. Show the possible future (4 min)

> "Here's what I think is possible. {{honest, grounded picture}}. It's real work, and I can't promise a specific outcome - but I'm confident in the process and in what tends to open up when someone does this kind of work with support."

## 5. Lane & path (4 min)

> "From what you've shared, this feels more like {{counseling - emotional/past work / coaching - goals/forward work}}. Does that match how you see it?"

Then the recommendation (see decision tree):

- **Package:** "I'd suggest we work in a {{6-session counseling package / coaching package}} - depth needs a bit of continuity."
- **Wedge (if confirmed):** "If you'd like to experience how I work and leave with a clear map first, there's a single Clarity Session - 90 minutes plus a written summary of the pattern we find."
- **Single:** "If it's really this one thing, a single session might be enough to start."

## 6. Practical & close (3 min)

> "Practically: sessions are 60 minutes, online, in {{English/German/Ukrainian/Russian}}. {{Price per chosen path}}, paid on booking. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before."

Close with freedom:

> "Take whatever time you need to feel into it. If it feels right, I'll send you everything to get started. If not, that's completely okay - no pressure at all. Either way I'll send a short summary of what we talked about and the link."

## After the call

- [ ] Send the recommendation + discovery/booking link (same day or next)
- [ ] If wedge delivered later → schedule the day-7 follow-up
- [ ] Note the lane and recommendation

## So what

Understand → name precisely → show possibility → recommend clearly → close with freedom. The pattern-naming step does most of the work. Follow-up emails: [05-client-emails.md](05-client-emails.md).
