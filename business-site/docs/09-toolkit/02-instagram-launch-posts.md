## 1 - Introduction / positioning

**Format:** Single image or short carousel (portrait + intro). Lane: both.

> You can understand yourself completely - and still feel stuck.
>
> I'm Anna. I'm a medical doctor, psychologist, and coach, and I work with sensitive, thoughtful people who have already read the books, done the reflecting, named their patterns - and still can't seem to move.
>
> Here's what I've learned, in myself and with the people I work with: understanding is not the same as release. Insight tells you *why*. It rarely changes the thing your body has been doing for years.
>
> That gap - between knowing and changing - is exactly where I work.
>
> Counseling for what you carry. Coaching for where you're going. Online, in English, German, Ukrainian, or Russian.
>
> If something here feels familiar, the link in my bio is a quiet place to start.

## 2 - The core thesis

**Format:** Carousel (one line per slide). Lane: both.

> "I know exactly what my problem is. So why can't I fix it?"
>
> Because insight and change live in different places.
>
> You can explain your pattern perfectly. Trace it to childhood. Name it in three languages. And still feel it run you on a Tuesday afternoon.
>
> That's not failure. That's the difference between understanding something with your mind and releasing it from your body.

**Format:** Single image. Lane: coaching.

## 5 - Burnout / "successful but empty"

**Format:** Carousel. Lane: counseling.

> Burnout doesn't always look like you can't get out of bed.
>
> Sometimes you still deliver. Still smile in meetings. Still answer emails at midnight.
>
> The collapse is quieter. It sounds like *"I don't know who I am without achieving"* - or *"nothing I win feels like enough anymore."*
>
> That's not a motivation problem. It's often a nervous system refusing to keep performing a life that costs too much on the inside.
>
> If a quiet Sunday fills you with dread instead of peace, that's worth listening to. → bio.

## 9 - Counseling vs coaching (self-select)

**Format:** Carousel (comparison). Lane: both.

> Not sure if you need counseling or coaching?
>
> Counseling is for what you carry - emotional weight, the past, patterns that hurt. *Are my issues emotional or past-focused?*
>
> Coaching is for where you're going - goals, transitions, the life you can sense but can't quite reach. *Are my issues goal or future-focused?*
>
> And if you genuinely don't know? That's okay. Clarity itself can be the goal - that's part of what a first conversation is for. → bio.
