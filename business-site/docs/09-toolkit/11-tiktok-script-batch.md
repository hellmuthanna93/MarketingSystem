# TikTok / Reel Scripts (10)

Ten short-form video scripts (30–60s) in Anna's voice. Each: a hook (first 2 seconds), a body (the insight), and a soft close. Talking-head or simple text-on-screen. Same scripts work for Instagram Reels.

> Speak naturally — these are scaffolds, not teleprompter copy. Humanizer pass before recording. No cure claims; soft CTA only.

---

## 1 — Understanding isn't enough

- **Hook:** "You can understand your problem completely and still be stuck. Here's why."
- **Body:** "Insight lives in your mind. The pattern lives in your body. Knowing *why* you brace before a meeting doesn't stop the bracing. That's not failure — it's just that understanding and release are two different things."
- **Close:** "If you've figured yourself out and still feel stuck, that gap is exactly where the real work is."

## 2 — Burnout doesn't look like burnout

- **Hook:** "High-achiever burnout doesn't look like burnout."
- **Body:** "You still deliver. Still smile in meetings. Still answer emails at midnight. The collapse is quieter — it sounds like 'nothing I achieve feels like enough.' That's not a motivation problem. It's a nervous system performing a life that costs too much inside."
- **Close:** "If a quiet Sunday fills you with dread, that's worth listening to."

## 3 — The procrastination reframe

- **Hook:** "You're not procrastinating because you're lazy."
- **Body:** "Part of you might be afraid that succeeding will change how people see you — or who you'd have to become. That's why starting feels heavier than the task itself. It's a quiet, protective 'no' underneath the frustrated 'yes.'"
- **Close:** "Find that 'no,' understand it, and you can finally move."

## 4 — "Just think positive"

- **Hook:** "If 'just think positive' worked, you'd have done it by now."
- **Body:** "Sensitive, thoughtful people don't get stuck from thinking too negatively. They get stuck from thinking *deeply.* You don't need to be talked out of your inner world — you need someone to go into it with you."
- **Close:** "That's the opposite of good-vibes-only. It's the real thing."

## 5 — Shame

- **Hook:** "Shame is the quietest feeling — and the most convincing."
- **Body:** "It whispers, 'if they really knew you, they'd leave.' So you perform, you stay a little hidden. But shame can't survive being met with warmth. It loses its grip when you're seen and nothing bad happens."
- **Close:** "You were never as unlovable as the shame told you."

## 6 — The anxious alarm

- **Hook:** "If you panic when someone gets distant, it's probably not about them."
- **Body:** "It's an old alarm that learned, early, that connection could vanish without warning. So a slow reply can flood you — not because you're too much, but because a younger part is bracing for a loss it couldn't survive."
- **Close:** "That alarm can be soothed. By the work, not the right partner."

## 7 — Ready doesn't come first

- **Hook:** "You're waiting to feel ready. It doesn't work like that."
- **Body:** "Confidence arrives *after* you act, not before. The people who look fearless just learned to move while afraid and let the confidence catch up."
- **Close:** "You don't need to feel ready. You need one honest step."

## 8 — The in-between

- **Hook:** "The old life doesn't fit and the new one hasn't arrived. That's not a problem."
- **Body:** "Most people rush to fill that empty space — grab the next plan just to escape the discomfort. But that space is where real change happens. Stay in it a little longer and you choose your next chapter instead of defaulting into it."
- **Close:** "The discomfort is the doorway."

## 9 — Inherited goals

- **Hook:** "You can hit every goal and still feel like you're living someone else's life."
- **Body:** "That happens when the goals were inherited — from parents, culture, an old you — and never questioned. The satisfaction never comes because the wanting was never really yours."
- **Close:** "Ask the uncomfortable question: if no one were watching, what would I build?"

## 10 — Reaching out is the hard part

- **Hook:** "The hardest part isn't the work. It's reaching out."
- **Body:** "So make the first step small: a free 30-minute call. Not therapy, not a pitch — just a conversation to understand what's going on and whether we fit. You can ask anything and decide nothing."
- **Close:** "Link's in my bio, whenever you're ready. No rush."
