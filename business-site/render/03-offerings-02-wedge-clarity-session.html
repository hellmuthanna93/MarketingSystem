What we do together

In one focused conversation, we find the pattern that's actually keeping you stuck - not the surface story, but the loop underneath. Afterward, I send you a written summary you can return to: what we named, what keeps it locked, where it shows up, and a clear next step.

How it works

  1. A short intake form so I can be present from the first minute
  2. Our 90-minute session: recognition, then the real pattern, then what's possible
  3. Your written pattern summary, delivered within 72 hours
  4. A gentle follow-up a week later - only if you'd like to go further

What you leave with

  • The pattern, named precisely enough that it finally makes sense
  • An understanding of what's been keeping it locked
  • A clear, honest next step - with no pressure to take it

Is it right for you?

The Clarity Session is for thoughtful people who want depth without a big commitment yet. It isn't a quick fix, and it isn't crisis care. If what you need is ongoing support, a package may suit you better - and we can talk about that too.
