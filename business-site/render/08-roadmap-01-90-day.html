90-Day Plan

A growth-and-consistency plan for the practice: get consistent, get visible, get systematic - each phase ending in shippable proof. Print this and tick as you go.

Phase 1 - Foundation

Days 1-30 · consistency + wedge design

  • Confirm the Clarity Session: name, price (€250-€300), promise
  • Set up the wedge: intake form, summary template, booking + payment
  • Establish 1 counseling + 1 coaching post/week rhythm
  • Batch-write and schedule the 12 Instagram launch posts
  • Ensure discovery-call CTA is on every page + bio
  • Confirm legal scope + agreement reviewed

Exit: posting consistently; wedge ready to sell; CTAs frictionless.

Phase 2 - Visibility

Days 31-60 · show up + convert

  • Publish the 12 LinkedIn launch posts
  • Run the 90-day content calendar
  • Publish 2 blog posts; repurpose into social
  • Use the discovery-call script consistently
  • Offer the wedge where it fits; track conversion
  • Start newsletter signup (double opt-in) + first lead magnet idea

Exit: discovery calls weekly; first wedge clients delivered.

Phase 3 - Momentum

Days 61-90 · systematize + deepen

  • Nurture 1-2 referral relationships
  • Send renewal invitations to package clients nearing completion
  • Collect 1-2 anonymous, opt-in testimonials
  • Publish first multilingual posts (DE / UK / RU)
  • Review KPIs and adjust
  • Protect the 2 free days/week - confirm sustainable rhythm

Exit: systems in place; wedge validated; content compounding; rhythm held.
