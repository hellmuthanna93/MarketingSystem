Instagram (primary)

  • Feed posts and carousels in brand template (1080×1080; see brand/layouts.md)
  • Stories for behind-the-practice warmth and soft invitations
  • Reels for reach; repurpose insights into 30-60s scripts
  • Bilingual where it fits (EN primary; DE / UK / RU for resonance)
  • CTA: discovery call link in bio; soft invitations only

LinkedIn (authority)

  • Same insights, slightly longer development
  • Professional ICP (high-functioning, burnout-prone)
  • Authority without posturing - lived perspective, not credential flexing

Blog / SEO

  • Existing posts set the bar (burnout, counseling-vs-coaching, expat counseling, signs-you-need-support)
  • Movement-first openings; internal links to service pages and discovery call
  • Seeds the repurposing pipeline (see 02-content-engine.md)

Newsletter (to build - TBD)

  • Owned audience insulates against algorithm risk
  • Double opt-in required (GDPR - see 06-outreach-compliance-germany.md)
  • One theme per issue; personal lens; soft CTA
  • Templates: ../09-toolkit/12-newsletter-templates.md

TikTok / Facebook

  • TikTok: experimental; same reel scripts
  • Facebook: maintain presence, low effort

Multilingual approach

EN is the default. Use DE / UK / RU where it deepens resonance with a segment (e.g. Ukrainian/Russian for that community, German for local reach). Adapt idioms, don't translate word-for-word. Test Cyrillic and long German compounds in templates. Detail: 09-international-considerations.md.

So what

Instagram + LinkedIn carry the present; blog/SEO and a future newsletter build the compounding, algorithm-resilient base. One insight feeds all of them. Lead gen mechanics: 04-lead-generation.md.
