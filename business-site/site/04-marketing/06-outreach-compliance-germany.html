Outreach & Advertising Compliance (Germany / DACH)

Not legal advice. Practical guardrails so marketing stays compliant in Germany. Confirm specifics with a Rechtsanwalt. Operational version: dach-mental-health-compliance skill.

Why this matters

Anna is a medical doctor offering counseling/coaching. Three regimes apply: UWG (unfair competition / unsolicited contact), HWG (advertising for health services), and GDPR/DSGVO (data, especially health data). Getting these right protects the practice and the brand.

UWG §7 - unsolicited contact

  • No cold email or cold calls to individuals without prior explicit consent (opt-in).
  • No cold DMs that amount to advertising to people who haven't engaged.
  • Newsletter requires double opt-in with clear consent and a working unsubscribe.
  • Warm/inbound only: people who follow, comment, message, or request contact.

HWG (Heilmittelwerbegesetz) - health advertising

Anna's medical status raises HWG sensitivity. Avoid:

  • Before/after promises or guaranteed healing
  • Fear-based advertising ("if you don't act, you'll get worse")
  • Misleading success/cure claims
  • Testimonials/patient stories that claim cure of a specific illness (HWG §11 historically restricts certain Werbung with recommendations and case histories for medical advertising)

Do instead:

  • Frame as counseling/coaching support and accompaniment, possibility and experience language
  • Keep testimonials about experience and process, anonymous, no disease-cure claims
  • When a claim feels health-clinical, soften and flag for legal review

Scope: Beratung vs Psychotherapie

  • Market as psychological counseling (Beratung) and coaching, NOT Psychotherapie (a protected activity), unless the legal basis is confirmed (see ../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md).
  • Symptom language is fine as audience recognition in content; copy must not promise to treat or cure clinical conditions.

GDPR / DSGVO - health data is special category

  • Health data = Art. 9 special category; explicit consent + strong safeguards.
  • Tools touching client data (calendar, video, notes, payment, email) need an Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV/DPA); prefer EU-hosted / DPA-covered providers.
  • Privacy policy must state what's collected, why, retention, and rights.
  • Never quote identifiable client material.

Quick gate for any campaign or post

  1. Implies Psychotherapie/treatment/cure? → reframe or flag
  2. Guarantees an outcome? → remove
  3. Uses a patient story to claim a cure? → reframe to anonymous experience
  4. Outreach to someone who didn't opt in? → don't send
  5. Tool touches client data without a DPA? → flag

So what

Inbound + warm-only outreach, possibility-not-cure language, anonymous testimonials, and DPA-covered tools keep the practice compliant and on-brand. International (non-DACH) notes: 09-international-considerations.md. Warm outreach mechanics: 07-outreach-plan.md.
