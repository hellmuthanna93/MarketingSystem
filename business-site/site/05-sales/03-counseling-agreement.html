Counseling & Coaching Agreement

Not legal advice. Outlines what a German-compliant Beratung/coaching agreement should cover. Have a Rechtsanwalt review before use. Fill-in template: ../09-toolkit/07-counseling-agreement-template.md.

Why a written agreement

Clarity protects both sides: scope, money, cancellation, confidentiality, and the honest limits of counseling/coaching (not Psychotherapie). It also documents the Beratung framing that keeps the practice within scope (see ../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md).

What it must cover

Section Content
Parties Anna Hellmuth (practice) and client
Service description Psychological counseling (Beratung) and/or coaching — explicitly not Psychotherapie / not medical treatment
Scope & limits No diagnosis, no treatment of illness, no guaranteed outcomes; referral if needs exceed scope
Format Online, 60-min sessions (90 for Clarity Session); platform
Fees & payment Per price list; payment on booking
Cancellation Free up to 48h before; full fee within 48h
Confidentiality What is kept private; lawful exceptions
Data protection GDPR/DSGVO basis; tools used; consent for health data
Client responsibilities Engagement, integration; emergencies → emergency services, not Anna
Crisis clause Counseling is not crisis care; signpost emergency resources
Term & termination How either party ends the engagement
Governing law Germany

Scope statement (the critical bit)

The agreement must clearly state the service is counseling/coaching support, not Psychotherapie under the Psychotherapeutengesetz and not medical treatment, and that Anna will refer out when a client's needs exceed this scope (e.g. acute clinical conditions, crisis).

Crisis & safety

Include a clear line that the service is not emergency care and direct clients to appropriate emergency resources (e.g. 112 / local crisis lines) if at risk.

So what

A clean Beratung/coaching agreement protects clients, keeps Anna in scope, and satisfies GDPR — turn the checklist into the fill-in template (../09-toolkit/07-counseling-agreement-template.md) and have it lawyer-reviewed. Onboarding flow: 04-onboarding.md.
