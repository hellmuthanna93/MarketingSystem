Finance Model

Illustrative model showing how the €300k goal is reachable at low volume. Figures use current prices; treat as planning scenarios, not guarantees. Confirm tax treatment with a Steuerberater (01-legal-setup-germany.md).

The constraint

Anna's time is the scarce resource: ≤6–7 working hours/day, 2 free days/week. So revenue must come from value per client, not client count. The model is built around package mix and price, not session volume.

Revenue building blocks (current prices)

Offer Price Per-session
Counseling single €120 €120
Counseling 6-pack €660 €110
Coaching single €180 €180
Coaching 6-pack (Basic Breakthrough) €1,020 €170
Coaching 12-pack (Profound Transformation) €1,920 €160
Clarity Session wedge (TBD) €250–€300 —

Capacity frame

Assume ~3 working days dedicated to sessions (rest for content, delivery prep, admin), well within the hour limit. At a sustainable depth pace, that supports a limited number of session-hours/week — which is exactly why price/mix must do the heavy lifting.

Illustrative path to €300k/year

€300k ≈ €25k/month. Some mixes that approach it (illustrative):

Mix (per month) Approx. revenue
~13 coaching 12-packs spread over their duration... — see note
Blended: e.g. 6 coaching 12-packs sold/quarter + steady counseling 6-packs + wedge flow trends toward target as pipeline matures

Note: a 12-pack (€1,920) is delivered over weeks, so "packages sold per month" and "revenue recognized per month" differ. The practical lever: sell enough higher-value coaching packages and counseling packages each month that recognized revenue averages ~€25k, while keeping session-hours within the limit. This requires premium pricing and likely price increases over time (see 02-transition-to-fulltime.md).

Why the wedge matters financially

The Clarity Session (€250–€300) monetizes prospects who aren't ready for a package and converts a share of them into packages — raising average revenue per lead without adding raw volume.

Cost structure (lean)

Cost Nature
Tools (video, payment, notes, newsletter, forms) Low monthly
Insurance (Berufshaftpflicht) Annual
Steuerberater / legal Periodic
Website / hosting Low
Optional: assistant / paid promotion Future
Taxes (income; USt where applicable) Per 01-legal-setup-germany.md

Low overhead means a high share of revenue is margin — the model's strength.

Levers (in priority order)

  1. Price — raise rates as demand/reputation grow
  2. Mix — shift toward coaching 12-packs
  3. Wedge conversion — more leads → packages
  4. Consistency — predictable pipeline reduces feast/famine
  5. Leverage (later) — products that earn outside session hours

So what

€300k at low volume is feasible only via premium pricing + package mix + wedge conversion — never via more hours. The model says: protect the hours, raise the value. Scenarios over time: 05-financial-projections.md.
