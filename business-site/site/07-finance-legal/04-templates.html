German invoice (Rechnung) must include

Per §14 UStG (and §19 note if Kleinunternehmer):

  • [ ] Full name and address of Anna (provider) and the client
  • [ ] Steuernummer or USt-IdNr.
  • [ ] Invoice date and a unique sequential invoice number
  • [ ] Quantity and description of the service (e.g. "Psychologische Beratung, 60 Min.")
  • [ ] Date/period of service
  • [ ] Net amount, USt rate and amount — or the §19 UStG small-business note if applicable (no USt shown)
  • [ ] Total amount
  • [ ] Payment terms / reference

Confirm USt handling per service with a Steuerberater (01-legal-setup-germany.md) — therapeutic vs coaching portions may differ.

Retention

Keep invoices and tax-relevant documents per German retention rules (commonly up to 10 years); store securely.

So what

A short, complete document set — invoice, agreement, intake, privacy, Impressum, DPAs — covers the practice's legal/financial paperwork. Templates make them repeatable; a professional review makes them safe. Projections: 05-financial-projections.md.
