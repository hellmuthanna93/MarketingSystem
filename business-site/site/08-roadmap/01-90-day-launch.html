90-Day Plan

A phased, executable plan to consistently fill the practice and validate the wedge. Printable version: ../../render/08-roadmap-01-90-day.html. Tasks roll up into 04-task-backlog.md.

This is a growth/consistency plan for an already-operating practice (online since 2023) - not a from-zero launch.

Phase 1 - Foundation (Days 1-30): consistency + wedge design

Goal: a reliable content rhythm and a confirmed wedge offer.

  • [ ] Confirm the Clarity Session wedge: name, price (€250-€300), promise (../03-offerings/02-wedge-offer-clarity-session.md)
  • [ ] Set up the wedge: intake form, summary template, booking + payment
  • [ ] Establish content rhythm: 1 counseling + 1 coaching post/week (../04-marketing/02-content-engine.md)
  • [ ] Batch-write and schedule the 12 IG launch posts (../09-toolkit/02-instagram-launch-posts.md)
  • [ ] Ensure discovery-call CTA is on every site page + bio (../04-marketing/05-website-plan.md)
  • [ ] Confirm legal scope + agreement reviewed (../07-finance-legal/01-legal-setup-germany.md)

Exit: posting consistently; wedge ready to sell; CTAs frictionless.

Phase 2 - Visibility (Days 31-60): show up + convert

Goal: steady discovery calls from compounding content.

  • [ ] Publish the 12 LinkedIn launch posts (../09-toolkit/03-linkedin-launch-posts.md)
  • [ ] Run the 90-day content calendar (../09-toolkit/04-content-calendar-90day.md)
  • [ ] Publish 2 blog posts; repurpose into social (../09-toolkit/13-blog-post-batch.md)
  • [ ] Use the discovery-call script consistently (../09-toolkit/01-discovery-call-script.md)
  • [ ] Offer the wedge in discovery calls where it fits; track conversion
  • [ ] Start a newsletter signup (double opt-in) + first lead magnet idea (../04-marketing/04-lead-generation.md)

Exit: discovery calls happening weekly; first wedge clients delivered.

Phase 3 - Momentum (Days 61-90): systematize + deepen

Goal: repeatable systems and the first compounding signs.

  • [ ] Nurture 1-2 referral relationships (../04-marketing/07-outreach-plan.md)
  • [ ] Send renewal invitations to package clients nearing completion
  • [ ] Collect 1-2 anonymous, opt-in testimonials (../03-offerings/05-case-study-strategy.md)
  • [ ] Publish the first multilingual posts (DE / UK / RU) (../09-toolkit/14-multilingual-adaptations.md)
  • [ ] Review KPIs and adjust (03-milestones-and-kpis.md)
  • [ ] Protect the 2 free days/week - confirm the rhythm is sustainable

Exit: systems in place; wedge validated; content compounding; sustainable rhythm held.

Success at day 90

  • Consistent 1+1 weekly posts sustained for 12 weeks
  • Wedge confirmed, sold, and delivered to first clients
  • Discovery calls happening weekly without paid ads
  • At least one newsletter capture live
  • Free days protected

So what

Three phases - get consistent, get visible, get systematic - each ending in shippable proof. Granular tasks: 04-task-backlog.md. Metrics: 03-milestones-and-kpis.md.
