Instagram Launch Posts (12)

Twelve ready-to-publish posts to (re)introduce Anna and the practice. Each is full copy in Anna's voice. Caption is below each; suggested visual/format noted. Soft CTA only; primary link in bio.

Voice check done against practice-voice. Run a final humanizer pass and swap {{...}} before posting. Wedge references are TBD until confirmed. Visual templates: projects/social/templates/, 1080×1080.

1 - Introduction / positioning

Format: Single image or short carousel (portrait + intro). Lane: both.

You can understand yourself completely - and still feel stuck.

I'm Anna. I'm a medical doctor, psychologist, and coach, and I work with sensitive, thoughtful people who have already read the books, done the reflecting, named their patterns - and still can't seem to move.

Here's what I've learned, in myself and with the people I work with: understanding is not the same as release. Insight tells you why. It rarely changes the thing your body has been doing for years.

That gap - between knowing and changing - is exactly where I work.

Counseling for what you carry. Coaching for where you're going. Online, in English, German, Ukrainian, or Russian.

If something here feels familiar, the link in my bio is a quiet place to start.

2 - The core thesis

Format: Carousel (one line per slide). Lane: both.

"I know exactly what my problem is. So why can't I fix it?"

Because insight and change live in different places.

You can explain your pattern perfectly. Trace it to childhood. Name it in three languages. And still feel it run you on a Tuesday afternoon.

That's not failure. That's the difference between understanding something with your mind and releasing it from your body.

The work isn't more understanding. You have enough. The work is going deeper than analysis - to the part that's still bracing.

That's what I do with people. → bio.

3 - Counseling lens (recognition)

Format: Single image. Lane: counseling.

You think you should be over it by now.

Actually, your body is still holding what your mind tried to leave behind.

That's why certain moments still catch you off guard - a tone of voice, a Sunday evening, a goodbye that's a little too final. You're not being dramatic. You're carrying something that never got to fully land.

Counseling is where it gets to land - gently, with someone who won't rush you through it.

4 - Coaching lens (recognition)

Format: Single image. Lane: coaching.

You think you're procrastinating because you're lazy.

Actually, part of you is afraid that succeeding will change how people see you - or who you'd have to become.

That's why starting feels heavier than the task itself. It's not a discipline problem. It's a quiet, protective no underneath the loud, frustrated yes.

Coaching is where we find that no, understand it, and help you move anyway.

5 - Burnout / "successful but empty"

Format: Carousel. Lane: counseling.

Burnout doesn't always look like you can't get out of bed.

Sometimes you still deliver. Still smile in meetings. Still answer emails at midnight.

The collapse is quieter. It sounds like "I don't know who I am without achieving" - or "nothing I win feels like enough anymore."

That's not a motivation problem. It's often a nervous system refusing to keep performing a life that costs too much on the inside.

If a quiet Sunday fills you with dread instead of peace, that's worth listening to. → bio.

6 - For the expat / between countries

Format: Single image. Lane: counseling (Ukrainian/expat segment).

There's a specific kind of loneliness in building a life in a country that isn't where your heart learned to feel safe.

You function. You're capable. You might even be thriving on paper. And still - part of you is somewhere else, in a language that holds you differently.

You don't have to choose between the life you're building and the one you left. We can work with both - in English, German, Ukrainian, or Russian, whichever lets you be most honest.

7 - Myth-bust / reframe

Format: Carousel. Lane: both.

"Just think positive."

If that worked, you'd have done it years ago.

Sensitive, thoughtful people don't get stuck because they think too negatively. They get stuck because they think deeply - and feeling stuck is a signal, not a flaw.

You don't need to be talked out of your inner world. You need someone who can go into it with you and help you find the way through.

That's the opposite of "good vibes only." It's the real thing.

8 - How I work (depth + warmth)

Format: Single image. Lane: both.

I'm not the kind of professional who sits behind a clipboard and nods.

I've sat where my clients sit - surgery, my own burnout, the long road from treating bodies to understanding minds. I know the inside of stuck, not just the theory of it.

So the work is warm, but it has a backbone. I'll meet you with real compassion, and I'll stay steady as the one who's confident we can get you where you need to go.

Depth without coldness. Movement without hype.

9 - Counseling vs coaching (self-select)

Format: Carousel (comparison). Lane: both.

Not sure if you need counseling or coaching?

Counseling is for what you carry - emotional weight, the past, patterns that hurt. Are my issues emotional or past-focused?

Coaching is for where you're going - goals, transitions, the life you can sense but can't quite reach. Are my issues goal or future-focused?

And if you genuinely don't know? That's okay. Clarity itself can be the goal - that's part of what a first conversation is for. → bio.

10 - The discovery call (lower the barrier)

Format: Single image. Lane: both.

Reaching out is the hardest part. I know.

So the first step is small on purpose: a free 30-minute call. Not therapy. Not a sales pitch. Just a conversation to understand what's going on and see whether we're a good fit.

You can ask me anything. You can decide nothing. You can leave with a little more clarity than you had - which, sometimes, is exactly where it starts.

Link in bio when you're ready. No rush.

12 - Soft invitation / close

Format: Single image, warm. Lane: both.

You don't have to wait for "someday," struggling against the tide alone.

Whatever you're carrying or reaching for - it can shift. Not through a slogan, not overnight, but through real work with someone who understands the inside of it.

If any of this has felt like it was written about you, that's not a coincidence. It might be worth a conversation.
