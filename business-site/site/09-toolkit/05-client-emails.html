Client Emails

Lifecycle email templates in Anna's voice — warm, brief, no pressure. Replace {{placeholders}}. EN with a DE sign-off variant noted. Compliance + confidentiality per dach-mental-health-compliance and case-studies-anonymized.

Sign-off: Warmly, Anna (EN) / Herzlich, Anna (DE). Discovery/booking link: https://calendar.app.google/ueKb9RbyxWyC6zfK8

1 — Discovery call recommendation follow-up

When: same day / next day after the call.

Subject: After our conversation, {{name}}

Hi {{name}},

Thank you for trusting me with what's going on for you — that's not a small thing.

From what you shared, what stood out to me was {{one-line reflection of the pattern}}. Working in {{counseling / coaching}} feels like the right fit, and I'd suggest {{recommendation: e.g. a 6-session package}}, so the work has room to actually land.

There's no rush. Sit with it. If it feels right, reply here and I'll send everything to get started. If now isn't the time, that's completely okay too.

Warmly,
Anna

2 — Intake / booking confirmation

When: after they decide to start.

Subject: You're booked — what happens next

Hi {{name}},

I'm glad we're starting this work together. Here's everything you need:

  • First session: {{date/time}}, online ({{platform link}})
  • {{Package/price details}} — payment {{status/instructions}}
  • A short intake form: {{link}} (a few questions so I can be present and useful from minute one)

Nothing else to prepare. Come as you are.

Warmly,
Anna

3 — Pre-session reminder

When: ~24h before.

Subject: See you tomorrow, {{name}}

Hi {{name}},

Just a gentle reminder of our session tomorrow at {{time}}: {{link}}.

There's nothing you need to do beforehand. If something's been on your mind this week, you're welcome to bring it — and if your mind is blank, that's fine too. We'll find the thread together.

Warmly,
Anna

4 — Post-session check-in (first session)

When: after session 1. Optional.

Subject: A small reflection after today

Hi {{name}},

Thank you for today — for showing up honestly. That takes more than people give themselves credit for.

If it's useful, one thing to gently notice this week: {{light reflection prompt tied to the session}}. No homework, no pressure — just an invitation to pay attention.

See you next time.

Warmly,
Anna

5 — Package renewal invitation

When: near the end of a package.

Subject: Where you've come — and what's next

Hi {{name}},

We're coming to the end of our {{package}}, and I wanted to name how far you've moved: {{specific, honest progress — no exaggeration}}.

If you'd like to keep going, we can continue with {{next package}}. And if this feels like a natural place to pause, that's a good outcome too — the door stays open whenever you want to return.

No pressure either way. Just let me know what feels right.

Warmly,
Anna

6 — No-show / missed session

When: after a missed session.

Subject: We missed each other today

Hi {{name}},

We didn't connect at our session time today — I hope everything's okay.

Life happens, and sometimes the weeks we most need the space are the ones it's hardest to make. Whenever you're ready, here's the link to reschedule: {{link}}.

No judgment at all. I'm here when you are.

Warmly,
Anna

7 — Testimonial request (anonymous, opt-in)

When: after meaningful progress. Easy to decline.

Subject: A small, completely optional ask

Hi {{name}},

Working with you has been meaningful, and I'm grateful for it.

If you ever felt moved to share a few words about your experience, it would help others who are wondering whether this kind of work is for them. It would be posted anonymously — only ever about your experience and process, never identifying you.

And please — if this doesn't feel right, simply ignore this message. It changes nothing between us.

Warmly,
Anna

8 — Wedge → package follow-up (day 7)

When: 7 days after a Clarity Session. (Wedge TBD.)

Subject: Sitting with your map

Hi {{name}},

It's been a week since our Clarity Session. I hope the written summary has been something useful to return to.

If the pattern we named has stayed with you — and you feel ready to actually work with it — the natural next step is {{recommended package}}. We'd pick up exactly where the map left off.

No pressure, and no expiry on the door. Whenever it's right, just reply.

Warmly,
Anna

9 — Re-engagement (past client, opted-in)

When: quarterly, warm.

Subject: Thinking of you, {{name}}

Hi {{name}},

No agenda here — just a quiet check-in to say I hope life has been kind to you lately.

If anything has surfaced that you'd like support with, you know where I am. And if all is well, it's simply good to know you're out there doing the work in your own way.

Warmly,
Anna

10 — Referral out (out of scope)

When: when needs exceed counseling/coaching scope.

Subject: The right kind of support for this

Hi {{name}},

Thank you for being so open with me. Based on what you've described, I think the most caring thing I can do is point you toward {{appropriate professional/service}}, who is better placed to support you with this specifically.

This isn't a door closing — it's making sure you get exactly the right help. {{Resource/contact details, incl. emergency resources if relevant.}}

Take good care of yourself.

Warmly,
Anna
