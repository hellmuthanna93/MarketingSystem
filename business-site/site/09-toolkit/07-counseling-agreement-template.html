The Practitioner provides psychological counseling (Beratung) and/or coaching as selected: {{counseling / coaching / Clarity Session}}. These services are supportive and non-clinical. They are not Psychotherapie within the meaning of the Psychotherapeutengesetz and not medical treatment.

2. Scope and limits

  • No diagnosis or treatment of illness ("Krankheitswert") is provided.
  • No specific outcome is guaranteed.
  • The Practitioner will recommend referral to appropriate professionals if the Client's needs exceed the scope of counseling/coaching.
