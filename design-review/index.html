A business-aware design review, with proposed improvements

The brand and craft are genuinely strong. The biggest wins are not a redesign, they are tightening conversion consistency and closing a few credibility leaks, so the site does its one job: turn the right person into a booked discovery call.

Mode: Redesign, preserve Dials: Variance 6 · Motion 4 · Density 3 Pages reviewed: 7 templates

1. The business this design has to serve

Every recommendation below ladders up to one of these drivers, taken from your marketing and voice docs.

One conversion

Every page exists to move the right person to "Schedule your discovery call." That is the metric the design should optimize.

Premium, low volume

A high-value practice. The site must feel expensive and trustworthy. Polish and calm beat aggressive funneling.

Trust is the lever

"Finally, someone understands me." The design's job is to make a sensitive, discerning reader feel understood and safe.

Sell movement

Lead with relief, clarity, transformation. Credentials and methods support trust but never shout.

Sensitive audience

Self-aware professionals 25 to 45, often expat, multilingual. Calm and feminine, no hype, no pressure.

No-pressure sales

Reassurance is welcome. Urgency and scarcity tactics are off-brand and avoided throughout.

3. Prioritized roadmap

Priority is business impact times confidence. Effort is rough: S under half a day, M about a day, L multi-day.

P0  Do first: credibility and the one conversion

1

Fix the broken copy on Life Coaching

The "Do I really need a coach?" intro contains corrupted text ("Say it to be delightful are my momma..."). A premium practice cannot ship gibberish on a money page.

Effort: SCredibility
2

Standardize the primary CTA label everywhere

One phrase: "Schedule your discovery call." Retire "Book a discovery call", "Book a free 30 min session with me", "Let's discuss the plan". One clear action converts better and reads as a more confident brand.

Effort: SConversion
3

Unify the CTA destination and behavior

Today the same button goes three places: an in-page anchor, an external calendar in a new tab, and the contact page. Pick one model (recommended: land on the embedded scheduler) so intent converts in place.

Effort: MConversion
4

Add quiet reassurance under primary CTAs

"Free · 30 minutes · no obligation." Lowers hesitation for a cautious audience without breaking the no-pressure rule.

Effort: SConversion

P1  High value: trust, self-selection, scannability

5

Curate the testimonials

Lead with the bolded one-line transformation, full quote behind "read more" or a carousel, visible quote capped near 3 lines. The current wall is skimmed, not read. Curation reads as more premium.

Effort: MTrust
6

Add a calm trust strip

Qualifications (MD, psychologist, hypnotherapy in training), online since 2023, four languages, Location Independent Therapists. Factual, not boastful. Surfaced on home and service pages.

Effort: MTrust
7

Add a "Counseling vs Coaching" chooser

The "which is right for me?" fork is a core conversion decision. Promote it from inside an FAQ to a visible, visual chooser. The comparison already exists in your audience doc.

Effort: MSelf-selection
8

Tighten the home hero

Get the value prop plus one CTA into the first viewport; move the long reflective paragraphs to the section below. First impression decides whether a sensitive reader stays.

Effort: MConversion
9

Unify the hero system across pages

Home, counseling, coaching, and contact use four different hero patterns. One system with variants improves cohesion and maintainability.

Effort: MConsistency
10

SEO and sharing

Add ProfessionalService and Person structured data, Open Graph and Twitter cards, canonical tags. A discovery-led practice lives on being found and shared credibly.

Effort: MReach

P2  Polish and strategic

11

Multilingual plan

At minimum signal "sessions in your language" as a trust element; ideally localized German, Ukrainian, and Russian landing pages with hreflang. The ideal client is frequently Ukrainian, Russian, or German.

Effort: LGrowth
12

Reading comfort on long pages

Constrain body measure near 65 characters, break long runs with subheads and pull quotes, vary section layouts to avoid monotony, especially on life coaching.

Effort: MEngagement
13

Move inline styles into components

The pages carry many inline styles. Consolidating into the brand CSS makes future iteration faster and more consistent.

Effort: LMaintainability
14

Decide the public label

Hero says "creative & ambitious souls"; strategy prefers "sensitive, thoughtful souls." Decide and apply consistently. Needs your sign-off.

Effort: SPositioning
15

Imagery consistency

Ensure warm, premium, feminine photography across pages with consistent treatment and aspect ratios. Photography carries the premium feeling more than any CSS.

Effort: MBrand

Multilingual
