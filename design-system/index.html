Accordion / FAQ

Native <details> with custom +/− icon. Keyboard accessible by default.

Can I not address my inner issues on my own?

There are times when self-reliance is sufficient, and times when it's not. A skilled specialist can save you years of wasted time and unnecessary struggle.

What is the difference between counseling and coaching?

Counseling focuses on healing and the past; coaching on goals and the future. The discovery call helps determine which fits you.

Do you guarantee results?

No guarantees - the best outcomes come from engaged clients who integrate insights between sessions.
