# The Next Chapter - Pricing

**Status:** Working paid-pilot price confirmed  
**Created:** July 2, 2026

## Revised recommendation

Position the program as a premium specialist offer, not a bundle of discounted
sessions.

Because the program has not yet been sold or delivered in this form, begin with
a paid pilot rather than trying to establish the final premium price in
advance.

**Introductory paid-pilot price: €1,950 total**

Payment options:

- One payment of €1,950.
- Three monthly payments of €650.

The first payment is due when the client enrolls. The installment option is a
payment schedule for the complete program, not pay-as-you-go coaching.
Payments are made by direct bank transfer or Revolut transfer.

Use this price for the first two paid pilot clients. Present €1,950 as the
current program price, not as a dramatic discount from an invented higher
price. Explain privately that the program is in its first structured version
and that honest process feedback will help refine it. Feedback is expected;
public testimonials are not.

## What the price includes

- Fully 1:1 delivery.
- 12 core sessions of 60 minutes across 16 weeks.
- Eight weekly sessions followed by four sessions every two weeks.
- Personalized reflections, exercises, and pattern work.
- Live guided hypno-meditations during relevant sessions.
- One client message per week between sessions.
- Anna's reply within two business days.
- A personal transition plan without an artificial deadline.
- One complimentary 60-minute follow-up session one month after the core
  program.

Paid assessments such as CliftonStrengths are optional, not required, and
purchased separately by the client.

## Why €1,950 is appropriate for the pilot

The program contains 13 hours of direct 1:1 contact, including the follow-up,
plus preparation, personalized exercises, and up to 16 weeks of written
support. At €1,950, the direct-contact equivalent is €150 per
hour before accounting for the additional work.

This is not the ideal long-term economic position. It is a controlled,
temporary validation price that reduces the commitment barrier while the new
program builds its own evidence.

The RAUEN Coaching Market Analysis 2024 reported:

- An average coaching fee of €175.98 net per hour.
- An average coaching process of 11.76 hours.
- Simplified average total coaching costs of approximately €2,070 net.
- Program or package pricing in 22.77% of reported coaching arrangements.

The 2025 ICF Global Coaching Study reported a worldwide average of $234 for a
one-hour coaching session and found that fees generally rise with experience.
These figures combine different markets and client types, so they are
benchmarks rather than a price list for private clients.

## Relationship to retired offers

Anna has confirmed that the new program is the only coaching offer. Coaching
single sessions, Basic Breakthrough, and Profound Transformation are retired.

Do not describe the new program as 12 sessions plus extras. Lead with the
transition, the four phases, and the full 16-week support container.

## When to raise the price

Review the price after the first two completed pilot clients.

Consider moving to €2,400 to €2,700 when:

- The program has clear anonymous feedback and pattern-safe case material.
- The delivery process is repeatable.
- Anna understands which elements create the most value and how much
  non-session time delivery actually requires.
- Strong-fit prospects understand the offer and the pilot clients would
  recommend the experience.

Do not promise an automatic increase or use it as sales pressure.

## What the pilot must teach

The pilot is not only a cheaper first version. It should answer:

- Do the right clients recognize themselves in the problem?
- Will they commit to a 16-week process?
- Which part of the promise matters most to them?
- Where do clients need more or less support?
- How much preparation and written-support time does delivery actually take?
- Which exercises and hypno-meditations create meaningful movement?
- What words do clients use to describe the change?
- Does €1,950 create resistance, or is the real barrier trust, timing, or offer
  clarity?

Do not change the price after one person declines. Gather several real
conversations and separate price objections from weak fit or weak trust.
