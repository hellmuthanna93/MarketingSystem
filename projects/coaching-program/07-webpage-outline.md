# The Next Chapter Webpage Outline

**Status:** Implemented as the Life Coaching subpage  
**Created:** July 6, 2026  
**Primary URL:** `/en/lifecoaching.html`

## Page purpose

Help a thoughtful professional recognize their own situation before they are
asked to consider the program. The page should create three feelings in order:

1. "This is exactly what I am experiencing."
2. "There is a reason I am stuck, and change is possible."
3. "This is professional support I can trust with an important decision."

The page replaces the retired coaching offers at the existing Life Coaching
URL. Preserving the URL avoids an unnecessary SEO migration and keeps the
current navigation intact.

## Narrative structure

### Hero: the desired movement

**Job:** Introduce the name, category, and outcome within one viewport.

- Program name: The Next Chapter
- Descriptor: Life and career transition coaching
- Outcome: choose a direction you trust and begin building a life that fits
- Primary action: Schedule your discovery call
- Secondary action: See if this fits
- Visual: a professional standing beside an open doorway, looking into natural
  daylight

### Recognition: the life that no longer feels like yours

**Job:** Create precise self-recognition before explaining the program.

Core headline:

> Your life still works. It just no longer feels like yours.

The section names the conflict between outward stability and inward
dissatisfaction, then shows the different forms the transition may take.

### Mechanism: why another plan has not solved it

**Job:** Explain the value of Anna's psychological depth.

Core idea:

> A new plan cannot help if the old pattern still makes every decision.

The section connects insight, repeated planning, fear of losing stability, and
the old patterns shaping each new choice.

### Relief: understanding without blame

**Job:** Give the reader emotional relief and establish the first part of the
method.

The choices that built the current life once protected something important.
Understanding them makes a calm goodbye possible.

### Outcomes: what the client can leave with

**Job:** Make the program result concrete.

- A direction that feels like theirs.
- The ability to distinguish fear from a genuine mismatch.
- A transition already in motion.

The copy states credible results without promising that an entire relocation,
career change, or business launch will be completed in 16 weeks.

### Process: how the work unfolds

**Job:** Show that there is a thoughtful structure behind the promise.

- Understand the old chapter.
- Choose the new direction.
- Grow into the future self.
- Become stable and take first steps.

### Program rhythm: how support works in real life

**Job:** Explain the practical container after the value is understood.

- 12 private sessions across 16 weeks.
- Weekly sessions for eight weeks.
- Sessions in weeks 10, 12, 14, and 16.
- One written message each week.
- Reply within two business days.
- Personalized exercises and live hypno-meditations.
- Complimentary follow-up one month after completion.

### Professional trust: why Anna

**Job:** Show expertise without leading with credentials.

The section introduces Anna as a medical doctor, psychologist, and coach after
the reader understands the problem and method. It explains the combination of
psychological depth and practical future-focused support.

### Investment

**Job:** Present the complete price calmly and clearly.

- €1,950.
- One payment or three monthly installments of €650.
- Direct bank transfer or Revolut transfer.

### FAQ

**Job:** Answer search-oriented and decision-stage questions.

- What if I do not know what career or direction I want?
- What if I am afraid to lose financial stability?
- Is this career advice or job-search support?
- How is coaching different from psychological counseling?
- Do I have to use an assessment?

### Closing invitation

**Job:** Lower the emotional threshold for the next step.

The reader does not need to know the complete path before asking for support.
The discovery call is framed as a calm mutual-fit conversation.

## Visual direction

- Preserve Source Serif 4 and the burgundy and cream brand system.
- Use light and cream surfaces with one deliberate burgundy relief section.
- Use asymmetric grids for recognition, outcomes, program support, expertise,
  and investment.
- Use 16px card radii, asymmetrical image masks, and pill buttons.
- Keep motion limited to existing scroll reveals and honor reduced-motion
  preferences.
- Collapse every grid to one column on mobile.
