# Coaching Program: Working Context

**Created:** July 2, 2026  
**Status:** Discovery and offer definition  
**Owner:** Anna Hellmuth

## Why this project exists

Creating a **specific, sellable coaching program** is a Q3 2026 goal and a July
priority. The immediate aim is to turn Anna's broad life-coaching offer into a
clearly defined program with a precise client, problem, outcome, structure,
scope, and price.

This folder is the working area for that new program. It does not replace the
canonical business-suite or brand documentation.

## Confirmed practice context

- **Practice:** Anna Hellmuth Psychological Counseling and Life Coaching.
- **Delivery:** Online and location-independent.
- **Languages:** English, German, Ukrainian, and Russian, or a mix.
- **Positioning:** Psychological depth combined with coaching practicality,
  pattern recognition, and genuine warmth.
- **Shared audience pattern:** Sensitive, thoughtful, high-functioning people
  who understand themselves well but still feel stuck.
- **Coaching lane:** Future- and goal-oriented work, including transitions,
  identity change, authentic success, paralysis, procrastination, fear of
  failure or success, indecision, and blocked creativity.
- **Coaching method:** Individually tailored action plans and assignments
  between sessions.
- **Entry point:** A free 30-minute discovery call.

## Coaching offer decision

The working public name is:

> A 16-week coaching program for life and career transitions

Review the name once more before the webpage is published.

- Profound Transformation.

The current generated website still contains the retired coaching offers. Do
not publish those sections unchanged. Replace them with The Next Chapter once

## Working audience hypothesis

The current coaching audience is described as people aged 30 to 45 who are
often skilled professionals, thoughtful seekers, high-functioning doers, or
people navigating a transition. They may be successful on paper yet feel
disconnected from themselves, unable to act on what they know, or exhausted by
forcing themselves forward.

This is a starting hypothesis. The new program still needs a narrower ideal
client and a specific buying situation.

Confirmed program structure and inclusions:

- Fully 1:1 delivery.
- 12 core sessions of 60 minutes across 16 weeks.
- Weekly sessions in weeks 1 to 8.
- Sessions every two weeks in weeks 10, 12, 14, and 16.
- The client may write once per week between sessions.
- Anna replies within two business days.
- Guided hypno-meditations live during phase-three sessions to experience the
  future self. No recordings are included.
- CliftonStrengths and other paid assessments are optional recommendations,
  purchased separately by the client if they choose.
- One complimentary 60-minute follow-up session one month after completing the
  core program.

## Definition of a usable first version

The program is ready for copy and design when one page can state, without vague
language:

1. Who it is for.
2. What specific situation brings them to the program.
3. What changes through the work.
4. How the program works.
5. What is included and excluded.
6. How long it lasts and what it costs.
7. Why Anna is the right guide.
8. What the next step is.

## Guardrails

- Keep coaching distinct from clinical psychotherapy and acute-crisis care.
- Do not promise or guarantee outcomes.
- Do not invent credentials, prices, testimonials, or client stories.
- Use `TBD` for unconfirmed program details.
- Keep public language warm, adult-to-adult, precise, and free of motivational
  hype.
- Use **sensitive, thoughtful souls** as the preferred broad public-facing
  audience label; narrow it further for this program.
- The Clarity Session is proposed and not yet live. Do not present it as an
  available step unless Anna confirms it.
