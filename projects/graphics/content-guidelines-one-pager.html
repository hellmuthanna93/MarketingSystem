Content guidelines - Anna Hellmuth

Sell movement, not process · annahellmuth.com

Positioning

Help people create lasting inner change - not sell information. People buy movement: relief, hope, clarity, confidence, possibility, transformation.

Guiding principle: Become the person whose content makes ideal clients think: "Finally someone understands what I have been experiencing."

Ideal client

Highly educated professionals, creatives, entrepreneurs - functioning well externally, stuck internally. Public label: creative & ambitious souls.

Before posting - checklist

  • Relevance - real human experience?
  • Specificity - "That's me"?
  • Authenticity - do I believe this?
  • Emotional connection - feel seen?
  • Depth - beyond common advice?
  • Business value - demonstrates how I think?

Post formula

You think X.
Actually, Y is happening.
That is why Z feels so difficult.

Weekly rhythm

1 counseling + 1 coaching post per week. Consistency > volume.

Counseling topics

  • Emotional regulation, shame, self-worth
  • Attachment, boundaries
  • Childhood conditioning, internal conflicts

Coaching topics

  • Fear and action, purpose, identity
  • Life transitions, authentic success
  • Growth obstacles, self-leadership

Voice

From experience, as an equal - warm, professional, honest. Avoid academic, detached, or generic self-help tone.
