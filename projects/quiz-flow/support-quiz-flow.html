Counseling or coaching quiz flow

A visual map of how the quiz guides a visitor from their first need toward counseling, coaching, mixed discovery call, or outside support.

Counseling leaning Coaching leaning Mixed / unclear Outside support

Q1 Branching Decision

  • I feel emotionally overwhelmed: counseling +2, then main area question.
  • I need to make a career, business, or life direction decision: coaching +2, then energy question.
  • Both are true: no points, then main area question.
  • Complete crisis: emergency/local support result.

Q2 If Emotional Or Both: Main Area Of Dissatisfaction

This question appears after emotional overwhelm or both. Every answer then goes to the energy question.

What is the main area in your life that leaves you unsatisfied?

  • Relationship. I feel unhappy in my current relationship, lonely, or unable to build a stable relationship. Counseling +3.
  • Career, self-realisation, and money. I am unsatisfied with my job or business. It does not feel like a good fit, feels too stressful, and does not bring me joy. Coaching +2.
  • Myself. I criticise myself, do not like my body, how I look, or my personality traits. I do not enjoy being myself. Counseling +3.
  • All areas of my life do not look the way I would like them to. Everything does not feel right. Counseling +2.
  • Something else. Counseling +1.

Q3 Energy Question: Conditional Scoring

Q3 strengthens the direction that is already emerging before this question.

  • If the score before Q3 already leans coaching, “I am mostly stable...” adds coaching +3.
  • If the score before Q3 already leans coaching, “I am functioning...” adds coaching +3.
  • If the score before Q3 already leans counseling, “I am able to do only the most necessary things...” adds counseling +3.
  • If the score before Q3 already leans counseling, “Most of my day is filled with anxiety...” adds counseling +3.
  • If the score is tied before Q3, Q3 does not add extra conditional points. The next answers clarify the result.

Possible results

Psychological counseling

Appears when: emotional support, stabilization, trauma, relationship pain, anxiety, heaviness, or low capacity are stronger.

The Next Chapter coaching

Appears when: future direction, decisions, transition, action readiness, and between-session steps are stronger.

Mixed discovery call

Appears only when: counseling and coaching are almost tied, and the person repeatedly chooses unclear / both answers.

Please seek local or emergency support

You need more intense care. Online counseling or coaching may not be the most suitable first step right now. It may be important to reach out to someone who can support you more intensively - for example, a doctor, emergency service, crisis hotline, psychiatric service, local psychotherapist, or a trusted person nearby. Do not stay in this alone. Ask for help.

Current result rule

If outside support has any points, it wins first.

Mixed appears only when difference <= 1 and mixed >= 4.

Otherwise the higher score wins: counseling or coaching.

Signal Typical weight
Strong emotional burden Counseling +3
Clear life direction / transition Coaching +2 to +3
Can act but needs support Coaching +2, counseling +1
Both / unclear Capacity question routes toward coaching or counseling

Why mixed is rare now

Mixed no longer competes as a third service. It only catches truly balanced answers.

How the quiz guides

When both are true, the quiz asks about energy, stability, and ability to navigate life before choosing a path.

Where counseling wins

Low capacity, emotional depletion, numbness, trauma, relationship wounds, and stabilization needs.

Where coaching wins

Future access, readiness to act, concrete transition, direction, and willingness to experiment.

Feedback prompts

  • Does the first split feel human and clear?
  • Should “too emotionally depleted to begin” go straight to counseling, or ask one more question first?
  • Does coaching feel too dominant in the flow, or is that okay because the quiz catches action readiness?
  • Should the mixed result be even rarer, or is this level useful?
