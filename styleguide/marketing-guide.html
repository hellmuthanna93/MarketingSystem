Marketing & content guide

Strategic reference for annahellmuth.com - positioning, audience, content philosophy, and sales approach. Sell movement, not process. Make ideal clients feel: "Finally someone understands what I have been experiencing."

Positioning & vision

You are not selling information. You help people create lasting inner change - combining psychological depth, coaching practicality, pattern recognition, and genuine warmth.

Core marketing principle

People do not buy coaching. People buy movement: relief, hope, clarity, confidence, possibility, transformation. Always communicate the change - not the process, method, or session itself.

Content philosophy

The goal is not to sound smart. The goal is to make people feel: "She understands exactly what I experience."

Anna's personal content principles

The purpose of my content

My content should show how I think.

  • Make deep observations
  • Name things that other people can't articulate
  • Connect theory with real life
  • Explain the "why" behind patterns

Capture ideas immediately

Write or record content as soon as an idea appears:

  • After a client session
  • After I learn something
  • From my own stories and observations

Make my work obvious

Invite people to work with me clearly. People should think:

"This is manageable. This is exactly what I need help with."

Translate the value of my work into language people understand.

Communicate my values and beliefs

The main goal of my content is teaching and bringing value.

I want people not only to consume information but also to understand how I see the world.

Know who I'm speaking to

  • Deep thinkers and high achievers
  • Immigrants and people in transition
  • Successful people who don't feel fulfilled
  • People with internal blocks
  • People who want more satisfaction in life

The experience I want to create

  • Understand themselves and life more deeply
  • Exhale and realize that nothing is fundamentally wrong with them
  • Feel accepted and seen
  • Leave with useful ideas and practical tools
  • Naturally become interested in working with me

Reminders for myself

  • I am already deep enough. I don't need to overcomplicate things.
  • I am not my content. Creating content is simply one of my roles. It exists to serve my goals and my business, not to define who I am.
  • There are enough successful specialists whose English is worse than mine.

Personal content lens

Speak

  • From experience, not from above
  • As an equal, not as an authority figure
  • Professionally but warmly
  • Honestly without oversharing
  • With conviction without preaching

Avoid

  • Sounding detached or overly academic
  • Textbook tone
  • Generic self-help advice
  • Excessive caution that removes personality

Content creation checklist

Before posting, ask:

  • Relevance - Does this describe a real human experience?
  • Specificity - Could somebody think: "That's me"?
  • Authenticity - Do I genuinely believe this?
  • Emotional connection - Does it make the reader feel seen?
  • Depth - Does it go beyond common advice?
  • Business value - Would an ideal client want to work with me after reading?
